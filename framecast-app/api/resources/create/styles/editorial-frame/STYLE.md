# Editorial frame

A designed, magazine-like film. A thin editorial frame (corner labels, a counting timecode, a beat number) and hard cuts to flat colour fields make it feel crafted and premium.

## Look
- Flat colour fields that change per beat (a restrained palette of 2 or 3 brand colours plus off-white and near-black).
- The frame: small mono labels in the corners (project name, beat number such as 02 / 05, a timecode that actually counts), a hairline progress rule.
- Type: a clean sans for statements, a serif italic accent word per line, mono for labels.
- Content sits on a clear grid with generous margins; images, UI and characters are cut out or framed in simple shapes.

## Motion
- Hard cuts between beats, on the words or the music; within a beat, elements build with precise, short eases.
- Labels update with each cut (beat number, section name).
- One signature transition used consistently, for example a colour field wiping across, or a shape that grows into the next scene.
- Restrained: fewer, deliberate moves; nothing wobbles.

## Structure
1. Cold open: a question or problem in one line, beat 01.
2. Four or five beats of about 4 to 5 s: problem, product, features, proof.
3. A tagline beat.
4. A logo lockup on a clean field.

## Never
- Soft cross-fades between every beat.
- Busy gradients or glows; the look is flat and printed.
- Labels that do not change or a timecode that does not count.
