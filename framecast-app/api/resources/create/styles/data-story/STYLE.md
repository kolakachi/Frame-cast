# Data story

The number is the story. Figures count, bars grow and comparisons resolve, each with its own beat, so the viewer remembers one result.

## Look
- A clean grid on a light or dark neutral field; one strong colour reserved for the key figure, everything else muted.
- Charts built from simple shapes: bars, lines, dots, a big single number. Clear axis labels only where needed.
- Type: tabular figures for numbers (Inter or Space Grotesk), a mono for small labels, one display size for the hero number.

## Motion
- Numbers count up to their value and land with a small overshoot; bars and lines grow from their baseline in reading order.
- One comparison at a time: before vs after, us vs them, the change highlighted as it resolves.
- A marker, tooltip or highlight points at the figure that matters as it is spoken.
- Hold every key figure at least 1.5 s after it lands.

## Structure
1. Hook: the headline result, big, in the first 2 s (or the question it answers).
2. Evidence: 2 or 3 figures, each its own beat.
3. Meaning: what it means for the viewer, in one line.
4. Close: the source or product and call to action.

## Never
- Any number that is not in the approved facts or the supplied material. With no approved figures, do not use this style.
- Several charts moving at once.
- Decorative 3D charts or fake precision.
