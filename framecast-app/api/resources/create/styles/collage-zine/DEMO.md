# Collage / zine: the worked example

A 7 s square sneaker spot (example source in style-example/; its pictures are stand-ins). Learn its technique; do not repeat it.

- Structure: printed poster with a stamped label, torn cut to a sunburst hero, torn cut to a rapid card montage, torn cut to the end card.
- Opening: a silver-printed photo on flat red; the FAST LANE label slaps on with a tilt.
- Signature shot: the cut-out mascot slaps onto turning sunburst rays with confetti and a circular badge line.
- Camera path: none; the energy comes from cuts, slaps and the montage.
- Score shape: a swish on every tear, a pop on every slap, ticks under the montage.
- Ending: a halftone product print with the stamped name, held.

Weak spot to beat: the stand-in pictures are flat; real cut-outs of the product and people carry the look.
