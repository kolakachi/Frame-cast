# Collage / zine

A hand-made printed look: cut, torn, stamped and stuck down, cut fast like a hype edit.

## Look
- Flat poster colours (two or three loud ones: red, yellow, teal, pink) and off-white paper; no gradients except inside photos.
- Every picture is a cut-out: people, products and props cut from their background (the cutout tool), with a white die-cut border and a short shadow (WM.sticker). Photos are printed: silver, ink, duotone or halftone (WM.grade), never left as plain glossy stock.
- Type is stamped or stuck on: heavy condensed sans in a solid label, set slightly tilted (2 to 8 degrees); one circular badge line turning (WM.circleText) at most once.
- One shared style line for every bought picture, so they read as one campaign (the same print treatment, light and palette).

## Motion
- Cuts are torn paper (WM.tear: one to three strips in the brand colours) or hard cuts on the beat; never soft crossfades.
- Things slap on: stickers and labels land with a tilt and a small bounce, a beat apart.
- At least one rapid montage (WM.cards, every 2 to 3 frames): product variants, faces, details.
- A sunburst (WM.sunburst) or confetti (WM.confetti) for the one big moment, not throughout.
- Keep the energy arc: two or three peaks with a short held beat between them, and a held end card.

## Structure
1. Hook: a cut-out slams on in frame 1 with a stamped line (0 to 1.5 s).
2. Proof and variety: fast scenes, each one idea, torn-paper cuts between them.
3. The montage: products or people every few frames.
4. End card: the product cut-out, the name stamped, the offer or call to action, held 1.5 s or more.

## Never
- Glossy 3D, glass, glow or soft gradients.
- Uncut photos with their original backgrounds, or a picture without a print treatment.
- More than three loud colours, or text over a busy photo without a label behind it.
