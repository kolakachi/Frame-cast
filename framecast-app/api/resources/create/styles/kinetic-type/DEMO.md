# Kinetic type: the worked example

A 15 s WyvStudio hook (example source in style-example/). Learn its technique; do not repeat it.

- Structure: problem, turn, offer as a word stack: VIDEO? / SLOW. / HARD. / the relief line / the brand alone / the promise / formats / close.
- Opening: one word slams in huge on near-black in frame 1.
- Signature shot: the field flips to full orange and HARD. drops in heavy black, then cracks apart into the next line.
- Camera path: static, centred type; energy comes from scale, stretch and the field flips.
- Score shape: silent build.
- Ending: a held wordmark with the URL beneath.

Weak spot to beat: from 7 s on, lines mostly fade in centred; keep the transformations going to the end.
