# Neo-brutalism: the worked example

A 15 s vertical drop ad for Stomp, a sample sneaker brand used only as an example (source in style-example/). Its
product, prices and copy belong to that sample; never reuse them. Learn the technique; do not repeat it.

- Structure: NEW DROP slam, the shoe card with its price, three reason chips, the 50% OFF burst over a marquee, SHOP NOW pressed.
- Opening: a yellow field, the black-bordered NEW DROP block stamps in on frame 1 with a FREE SHIPPING sticker.
- Signature shot: the price block slams into its shadow while the old price is struck through.
- Camera path: static frame; energy comes from slams, tosses and the marquee.
- Ending: the SHOP NOW button pressed into its shadow with the brand block beside it.

Weak spot to beat: the chips land in the same rhythm; vary their timing with the voice.
