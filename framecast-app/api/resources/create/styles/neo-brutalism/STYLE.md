# Neo-brutalism

Loud and honest: flat blocks that slam into place, every edge outlined, nothing soft. It reads as confident and
handmade-digital, the look of a brand that doesn't hide behind gradients.

## Look
- Flat fills only: 3-4 bright brand colours (yellow, pink, lilac, mint, or the brand's own) plus black and white. No gradients, blur or glow.
- Every block has a 6-8 px black border and a hard offset shadow: 10-14 px down and right, solid black, no blur.
- Type: Archivo Black for headlines (huge, tight), Space Mono for labels, prices and small print. Text sits inside blocks, never floating.
- Stickers (rotated -6 to 8 degrees), a burst star for the offer, a marquee strip ("SALE ✦ SALE ✦") along an edge, a dotted or grid field behind.

## Motion
- Blocks slam in: WM.stamp for words and badges, WM.toss for cards, WM.pop for chips; a 1-2 frame overshoot, then dead still.
- Buttons get pressed (WM.press) and shadows collapse as they press (the block moves into its shadow).
- The marquee runs at a constant speed (linear is right here); everything else is snappy.
- A new element every 0.4-0.7 s; hold the offer card for at least 1.5 s.

## Structure
1. Hook: the drop or offer word, huge, in frame 1.
2. The product or app in a bordered card with its name and price.
3. 2-3 reasons as chips or stickers.
4. The offer burst and the call to action button, pressed.

## Never
- Soft shadows, gradients, glass, rounded pastel minimalism: that's another style.
- Prices, discounts or ratings that are not in the approved facts.
- More than one burst star on screen.
