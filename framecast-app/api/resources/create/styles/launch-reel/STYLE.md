# Launch reel

A keynote-style reveal. Darkness, light and depth build anticipation, then the product arrives as the hero.

## Look
- Deep dark background with a soft coloured glow and subtle depth (layered blurred shapes, a faint grid or particles).
- Glass and light: frosted panels, rim light on edges, a light sweep across surfaces.
- One hero object: the product UI, device or logo, lit as the brightest thing on screen.
- Type: a refined sans, medium weights, generous letter spacing for small labels; big words only for the reveal line.

## Motion
- Slow, confident camera: a push through layers, a parallax drift, a light sweep. Then one fast, decisive reveal.
- Tease before the reveal: glimpses, silhouettes, a single line of text.
- The reveal is the signature shot: the hero arrives with a burst of light, scale or depth, on the strongest musical hit.
- After the reveal, 2 or 3 quick feature flashes, then a held lockup.

## Structure
1. Tease: darkness, a line of intrigue, a glimpse (0 to 4 s).
2. Reveal: the hero arrives (around 35 to 45% through).
3. Flashes: 2 or 3 short feature beats.
4. Lockup: name, date or offer, held with the glow.

## Never
- Bright flat backgrounds, or many competing colours.
- Revealing everything in the first second.
- Glow and blur so heavy that text loses contrast.
