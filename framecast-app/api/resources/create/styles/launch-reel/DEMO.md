# Launch reel: the worked example

A 15 s WyvStudio teaser (example source in style-example/). Learn its technique; do not repeat it.

- Structure: tease, reveal, three quick flashes, lockup.
- Opening: a blinking cursor among drifting frosted glass panes.
- Signature shot: a light burst as the glass browser card snaps forward out of the blur.
- Camera path: a slow push through the panes, then a tilt and parallax on the card.
- Score shape: silent build.
- Ending: the glowing app mark with the name and URL, held.

Weak spot to beat: the first 3 s are nearly empty; the tease must move and intrigue from frame 1.
