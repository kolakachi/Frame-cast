# Isometric: the worked example

A 15 s vertical explainer of an online order reaching a door, for Parcelo, a sample delivery service used only as
an example (source in style-example/). Its copy belongs to that sample; never reuse it. Learn the technique; do not
repeat it.

- Structure: the phone where the order is placed, the warehouse, the van, the house, then a pull back over the whole path and the brand.
- Opening: the phone stands on the isometric ground, its screen lights and the parcel pops out of it.
- Signature shot: the camera glides with the glowing parcel from stop to stop, each label popping as it arrives.
- Camera path: one continuous glide down the diagonal, then a pull back.
- Ending: the whole path in view with PARCELO and the call to action.

Weak spot to beat: every stop gets the same hop; vary the reaction to what happens there.
