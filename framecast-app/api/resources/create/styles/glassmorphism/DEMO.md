# Glassmorphism: the worked example

A 15 s vertical showcase for Skye, a sample weather app used only as an example (source in style-example/). Its
place names, temperatures and copy belong to that sample; never reuse them. Learn the technique; do not repeat it.

- Structure: today's forecast card, the hourly strip coming into focus, a rain alert sliding down, the app name.
- Opening: the forecast card already floating over a drifting blue-violet field, the temperature counting up.
- Signature shot: the rack focus from the forecast to the hourly strip, the far card softening as the near one sharpens.
- Camera path: one slow drift upward across the whole video.
- Ending: SKYE on a glass pill with the call to action.

Weak spot to beat: the alert lands in the middle of the strip's beat; give each card its own moment.
