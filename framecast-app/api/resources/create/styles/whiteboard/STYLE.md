# Whiteboard explainer

Teaching on one board. Every idea is drawn as it is said, so the viewer watches the explanation being built and remembers the picture.

## Look
- One off-white board (#F7F6F2 or the brand's lightest neutral) for the whole video, never a cut to a new background.
- Simple marker drawings: single-weight strokes (6 to 9 px), round caps, no fills except a light highlighter wash. Icons are drawn, not pasted.
- Handwriting (Caveat) for labels and short lines; a clean sans (Inter) only for the brand and URL at the close.
- Black marker for everything; one emphasis marker (red or the brand accent) for circles, underlines, arrows and the key word.

## Motion
- Strokes draw on along their path (SVG `pathLength="1"`, dash offset 1 to 0), timed to the words they illustrate; handwriting writes on left to right.
- The camera travels along one board to the next drawing (ease in and out, 0.8 to 1.2 s); earlier drawings stay on the board.
- Emphasis is drawn: a circle around the cause, an underline under the key word, an arrow from problem to fix.
- Hold each finished drawing about 1 s before the camera moves on.

## Structure
1. Hook: the question or the problem, written on, with one drawing.
2. Explain: 2 or 3 steps or causes, each its own drawing and label, linked by arrows.
3. Turn: the key one circled, then the fix drawn beside it.
4. Close: the brand written on the board with an underline, and the call to action.

## Never
- Photos, gradients, drop shadows or 3D: it is a board.
- A drawing that appears whole: everything is drawn on.
- More than one emphasis colour, or emphasis on everything.
- Claims, numbers or advice that are not in the approved facts.
