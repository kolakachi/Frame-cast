# Whiteboard explainer: the worked example

A 15 s explainer for Fernhouse, a sample plant-care app used only as an example (source in style-example/). Its copy belongs to that sample; never reuse it. Learn the technique; do not repeat it.

- Structure: the question, three causes drawn in a row, the usual one circled, the fix with an arrow, the brand.
- Opening: the question writes on in handwriting while a wilting plant draws beside it.
- Signature shot: the camera glides down one continuous board; the red marker circles the cause and an arrow draws to the fix.
- Camera path: one tall board, four stops, eased glides between them; nothing is ever cut.
- Score shape: a light voice-led explainer (no music in the example).
- Ending: the brand handwritten on the board, underlined in red, with the URL.

Weak spot to beat: the three cause icons draw at the same pace; vary the rhythm with the voice and land the circle on the stressed word.
