Video ads don't collect money. Funnels do.

Your ad stops the scroll and they click. Then what? A slow, generic product page, a landing page that misses their problem, or an offer with no urgency and no upsell, and the click is wasted. With ad costs where they are, a strong video ad pointed at a weak page burns budget before it makes a sale.

The traps that kill most offers

  • No real demand: weeks building something nobody was going to buy.
  • The wrong price: too low to pay for the ads, or too high without the value to justify it.
  • The patchwork funnel: a page builder, a copywriter, a lead magnet, emails and checkout, all separate.
