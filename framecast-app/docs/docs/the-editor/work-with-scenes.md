# Work with scenes

Every video is a stack of scenes. The **Scenes** column (left) is where you manage them. Click a scene to select it and load its settings into the [config panel](/the-editor/project-settings-and-script).

## Add a scene

There are two ways to add a scene:

- **+ Add** at the top of the Scenes column — adds a scene.
- **+ Insert** on the divider under any scene — inserts a scene in that exact spot.

Both open the **Add New Scene** panel:

1. **Scene type** — Narration, Hook, Transition, Text Card, or Quote.
2. **Script** — write the scene's narration, or click **✦ AI Generate** to draft it.
3. **Visual source** — choose how the scene looks:
   - **Video** — a stock clip, background loop, or text-only card, matched to a search query.
   - **Image** — a stock still, matched to a search query.
   - **✦ AI** — an AI-generated image; optionally bind a [character](/characters/create-a-character), pick a style, and add a prompt override.
   - **Assets** — pick a file from your library.
4. Click **Add Scene**.

## Duplicate a scene

Click **Duplicate** on a scene card. A copy is added and selected, so you can quickly make a variation of a scene you like.

## Reorder scenes

Use the **↑** and **↓** arrows on each scene card to move it up or down. (You can also drag scene blocks in the [timeline](/the-editor/timeline).)

## Delete a scene

Click **Delete** on a scene card, then confirm in the **Delete this scene?** dialog.
