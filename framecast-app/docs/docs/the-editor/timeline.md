# The timeline

The **Timeline** gives you a track-based view of your whole video. Open it with the **Timeline** button in the top bar; close it with **×**.

## Tracks

The timeline shows five stacked tracks:

- **Scenes** — blocks labeled S01, S02… sized by duration. The active scene is highlighted.
- **Voice** — a waveform per scene.
- **Sounds** — the sound effect on each scene, or **+** to add one.
- **Music** — the project's background track (or "No music selected").
- **Captions** — a snippet of each scene's script.

## Interactions

- **Scrub** — click the ruler or drag the playhead to seek. Seeking switches the preview to **Full Video**.
- **Select a scene** — click its block to load it in the config panel.
- **Reorder scenes** — drag a scene block left or right; a drop indicator shows where it lands.
- **Add a sound** — click **+** on the Sounds track to open the sound picker.
- **Zoom** — **Fit**, **2×**, or **3×** to widen the tracks.

## Scene vs Full Video

The preview's **Scene / Full Video** toggle (under the player) changes what plays. In **Full Video** mode the scrubber shows scene-boundary markers, and the timeline playhead maps across the whole video.
