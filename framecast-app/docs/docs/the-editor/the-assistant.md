# The assistant

The **Assistant** edits your video from plain-language instructions. Instead of hunting through panels, you tell it what you want — *"change the music to upbeat indie pop"*, *"swap scene 2 to stock video"*, *"make the script punchier"* — and it proposes the change for you to approve.

Switch the right rail to **Assistant** (or press **⌘/Ctrl + J**).

## Set the scope

At the top, **Editing** lets you point the assistant at the **whole project** or a **specific scene**. Use **↺ New chat** to start a fresh conversation — your video changes are kept.

## Ask for a change

Type an instruction and press **⌘/Ctrl + Enter** (or **Send**). When the chat is empty, quick-prompt chips give you a head start:

- *change voice on this scene*
- *use upbeat music*
- *swap visual to stock*
- *rewrite this script punchier*
- *animate this scene*
- *add a CTA scene*

![the Assistant tab with a proposed action card](/img/howto/editor-assistant.png)

## Approve changes

The assistant replies with one or more **action cards**. Each card shows:

- What it will do, with a preview of the change.
- An estimated cost (**~N cr**) for anything that generates media.
- **Apply** to run it, or **Skip** to dismiss it.

When a card runs, you'll see progress; once applied, it shows what it spent and — for most actions — an **↩ Undo**. If a turn has several actions, you can **Apply all** to run them in sequence.

If a change costs more than your balance, the card shows **Top up →**.

## Auto-apply low-risk edits

The **Auto-apply low-risk** toggle (on by default) runs cheap, reversible edits immediately, while paid or structural changes still ask first. Turn it off to approve every change yourself.

## What the assistant can do

- Rewrite a scene's **script**, or change **captions**.
- Swap to **stock video/image**, **regenerate** an AI image, or **animate** a scene.
- **Re-record** a scene's voice or change the **music**.
- **Add**, **reorder**, or add a **sound effect** to scenes.
- **Apply a brand kit**, set an **audiogram**, or **lock the look** of a subject.
- Kick off an **export** or open the **schedule** flow.

## Defaults & creative brief

Two small controls fine-tune how the assistant works:

- **⚙ Defaults** — set a preferred **image model**, **animation tier**, and **visual source**. These are hints; your explicit words always win.
- **🎯 Theme & brief** — set the project's Theme, Topic, Visual style, Tone, and a recurring subject, so edits stay on-brand. **Save & lock** to keep them fixed.
