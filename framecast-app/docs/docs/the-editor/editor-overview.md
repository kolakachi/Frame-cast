# Editor overview

The editor is where every video comes together. You land here after generating a video (or right away if you [started from scratch](/make-a-video/from-scratch)). Everything is editable — swap a visual, change the voice, tweak captions, or just tell the [assistant](/the-editor/the-assistant) what you want.

## The layout

The editor has three columns plus a top bar:

- **Scenes** (left) — every scene in order. Add, duplicate, reorder, or delete scenes here. See [Work with scenes](/the-editor/work-with-scenes).
- **Preview** (center) — plays your video. Toggle between:
  - **Scene** — plays just the selected scene.
  - **Full Video** — plays the whole video, with scene markers on the scrubber.
- **Config / Assistant** (right) — the control panel. Two tabs:
  - **Config** — every setting for the selected scene and the project (script, visuals, voice, sounds, captions, music, brand kit).
  - **Assistant** — make changes in plain language. See [The assistant](/the-editor/the-assistant).

Switch the right rail between Config and Assistant with **⌘/Ctrl + J**.

## The top bar

- **Timeline** — open the [timeline](/the-editor/timeline) to see scenes, voice, sounds, music, and captions on tracks.
- **Variants** — open [video variants](/variants-and-hooks/video-variants) to generate alternate cuts.
- **Export / Open / Download / Schedule / Send for approval / Share** — see [Export, share & schedule](/the-editor/export-share-and-schedule).
- **Back to Dashboard**.
