# Export, share & schedule

When your video is ready, the top bar handles everything from here — exporting, downloading, sharing, scheduling, and sending for review. The Open, Download, Schedule, and Send-for-approval actions appear once an export has finished.

## Export

Click **Export** to open the **Export formats** menu and pick the aspect ratios to render:

- **9:16** — Reels, TikTok, Shorts
- **1:1** — Feed
- **4:5** — Instagram portrait
- **16:9** — YouTube

Your project's own ratio is pre-checked. **Each format is a separate render of the same video — no extra credits.** Click the export button to queue them; you'll see progress in the export pill (Export queued → Exporting → Export ready).

:::tip Before you can export
Every scene needs a script, a visual, and a generated voice. If something's missing, the export button is blocked and tells you which scene to fix.
:::

## Open & Download

- **Open ↗** — play the exported MP4 in a new tab.
- **Download ↓** — save the MP4 to your device.

## Share a public link

Click **🔗 Share publicly** to create a shareable link and copy it to your clipboard. It becomes **🔗 Copy share link** afterward, so you can re-copy it anytime. See [Share a sample link](/export-and-publish/share-a-sample-link).

## Schedule to social

Click **📅 Schedule** to open the scheduler. Pick the connected accounts, write per-platform captions (with an **AI caption** helper), and choose **Schedule for later**, **Publish now**, or **Save as draft**. Full details — connecting accounts, YouTube title/category/visibility, and the AI-content disclosure — are in [Connect & publish](/export-and-publish/connect-and-publish) and [How the calendar works](/calendar/how-the-calendar-works).

## Send for approval

Click **📝 Send for approval** to email a reviewer a private link — no WyvStudio account needed on their end.

1. Enter the **reviewer's email** (required), and optionally a **name** and a **note**.
2. Set **Expires in** — 3, 7, 14, or 30 days.
3. Click **Send link**. You'll get a copyable **review link** as well.
