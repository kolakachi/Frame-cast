# Add sounds & music

Two Config sections handle audio beyond the voice: **Sounds** (per scene) and **Music** (the whole project).

## Sounds (per scene)

Add a sound effect to the selected scene:

- **Pick Sound** — choose an effect from the library.
- **Volume** — 0–200%.
- **Change Sound** or remove it with **✕**.

Sounds are scoped to the single scene you're on.

## Music (whole project)

The **Music** section sets one background track across all scenes. Three tabs:

- **Library** — browse tracks, filtered by mood (All, Dark, Upbeat, Calm, Epic). Audition with ▶, or pick **No music**.
- **My Uploads** — use your own track from the media library.
- **✦ AI Generate** — describe a **mood or genre** (calm acoustic, upbeat indie pop, cinematic ambient, lo-fi chill, tense electronic, inspiring orchestral, warm folk, energetic synth, hopeful piano) or type your own, then generate a custom track. The cost is shown on the button.

### Music controls

- **Volume** — 0–100 (default 30).
- **Duck vol.** — how far music dips under the voice (0–50).
- **Fade in** — 0–2000 ms.
- **Loop track** — repeat the track to fill the video (on by default).
- **Duck voice** — automatically lower music when the voice speaks (on by default).
