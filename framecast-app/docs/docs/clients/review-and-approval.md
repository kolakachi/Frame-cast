# Review and approve a version

Approval applies to an **exported version**, not every future version of a project.

## Send work for review

1. Complete production and export the current video.
2. Use **Send for approval** in the available editor or UGC review controls.
3. Complete the reviewer details and send the review invitation. The UGC approval dialog emails the reviewer a unique link; they do not need a WyvStudio account.
4. Check the client's **Reviews** section for the version and its current status.

## Give actionable feedback

On the review page, watch the video, add timed comments where a change is needed, and choose **Reject** with feedback when changes are needed, or **Approve** when satisfied. A useful comment identifies the moment and desired change: “At 00:08, replace the closing line with ‘Explore the Acme range’.”

[![Reviewer comments attached to a specific moment in the exported video](/img/howto/clients-comments-guide.png)](/img/howto/clients-comments-guide.png)

Use **Attach current time** while reviewing, then **Add comment**. Posting a comment and submitting an approval decision are separate actions.

The agency follows the review link to see the comments and decision. If changes are requested, revise the project and **export the new version**. Request approval for that export; approval of the previous export is not approval of the new one.

## Why a video is missing from Delivery

The delivery builder lists exports with approval for that **exact export ID**. If nothing appears, check that the export completed and that its review is approved. A ready project, an old approval or a request marked delivered does not satisfy this requirement.

The next step is [Delivery and handoff](./delivery-and-handoff). The delivery page distributes approved work; it does not replace the review step.
