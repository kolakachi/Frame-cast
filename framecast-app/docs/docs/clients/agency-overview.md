# The Agency overview

On agency plans (Agency, Lifetime Agency, AppSumo Agency and Enterprise), the sidebar has one **Agency** item with three tabs:

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | See what needs you across all clients, and this month's credits |
| **Clients** | Manage your client workspaces — see [Set up a client workspace](./client-hub) |
| **Team** | Invite collaborators — see [Team and collaborators](./team-and-collaborators) |

## Needs you

**Overview** opens with **Needs you**: everything across your clients that is waiting on someone.

- **Changes a client asked for**, with the client's note.
- **Messages from clients**.
- **Approved videos not yet scheduled**.
- **Finished videos not yet sent to the client** for approval.
- **Videos waiting on the client**, with how many days they've been waiting.

Click an item to switch into that client's workspace and open the video.

## Credits this month

Below **Needs you**, **Credits this month** shows what was spent:

- **By client** — each client workspace.
- **By person** — each collaborator.
- What the **agency itself** used.

## What collaborators see

A [collaborator](./team-and-collaborators)'s **Overview** shows only the clients they're assigned to, plus how many credits they have left this month (for example, "400 of 1,000 credits left this month").
