# Invite people and manage access

Open **Access & budget → People**. Enter an email, choose an access level and click **Invite**.

[![People section with role controls, an accepted member and a pending invitation](/img/howto/clients-people-guide.png)](/img/howto/clients-people-guide.png)

*Select a screenshot to view it at full size. Screenshots use illustrative demo data.*

| Access level | Intended use |
|---|---|
| View only | View work, request videos and approve work |
| Edit | Make and change videos |
| Admin | Edit content and manage client brand settings |

These are roles within the client workspace. Client Admin is not agency-wide administrative access. Do not use it to grant access to other clients or agency billing.

## Check invitation status

The list distinguishes **invited**, **invite expired**, **email failed**, and **accessed workspace**. For a person who has not accessed the workspace, use the refresh icon to resend an invitation. Check the address first if delivery failed. A member who has already accessed the workspace does not need another invitation to sign in.

Use the role dropdown to change permissions. Use the remove **×** control to revoke membership; read the confirmation. Their work stays in the workspace. Inviting them again later is separate from restoring any previously revoked delivery link.

## What the client sees

Invited client roles land in their client-work area rather than the agency's client-management list. Explain that they should open the invitation, sign in using the invited address and confirm the workspace name. Their available production and editing controls depend on the assigned role.

If someone cannot get in, check invitation status, their email, role, and whether the client is paused or archived before sending repeated invitations.
