# Team and collaborators

A **collaborator** is a member of your agency's team — a freelancer or editor. They make and change videos using your agency's credits, in your agency workspace and in the clients you assign to them, up to a monthly allowance you set.

The agency owner invites and manages collaborators on **Agency › Team**.

## Invite a collaborator

1. Open **Agency** in the sidebar and choose the **Team** tab.
2. Enter their email. It must not already have a WyvStudio account.
3. Set a **monthly credit allowance**. Leave it empty for no limit.
4. Tick the **clients** they may work in.
5. Press **Send invitation**. They get a sign-in link by email, valid for **7 days**.

You can have up to **25 collaborators**.

## Change, pause or remove

- **Edit** — change their allowance or which clients they can work in.
- **Pause** — they can't sign in or spend credits while paused.
- **Remove** — they lose access. The videos they made stay.

## How the allowance works

- Each paid action is checked against what the collaborator has left this month. If an action would go over, it's refused with a message showing what's left.
- Work approved in one go is checked against the allowance when it's approved, so it never stops halfway.
- On **Agency › Overview**, **Credits this month** shows what each person spent.

## What collaborators can and can't see

Collaborators **can** make and change videos in the agency workspace and their assigned clients. Their **Agency › Overview** shows only their clients and how many credits they have left this month.

Collaborators **can't** see billing, API keys, client list management or the **Team** tab. The dashboard's studio setup steps are hidden for them.

## Collaborators vs. client seats

A collaborator works for your agency. A **client seat** is someone from the client you invite into one client workspace as View only, Edit or Admin — see [Invite people and manage access](./people-and-access).
