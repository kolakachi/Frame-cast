# Fund a client and control spending

Open **Access & budget** on the client detail page. Funding and caps solve different problems: funding decides **where credits come from**; a cap limits **how much can be spent this calendar month**.

[![Client credit allocation, monthly cap and people controls](/img/howto/clients-budget-guide.png)](/img/howto/clients-budget-guide.png)

*Select a screenshot to view it at full size. Screenshots use illustrative demo data.*

## Choose a funding mode

| Mode | How generation is paid for | What happens when credits run out |
|---|---|---|
| Shared / pooled | The client uses the agency's shared balance, subject to its cap | Work is limited by the remaining shared balance and cap |
| Funded | The client uses its allocated balance | It cannot fall back to the agency's shared balance |

To allocate credits, enter an amount and click **Add**. Transfers use **top-up credits only**; monthly credits stay in the shared balance. If you want clients to use monthly credits, leave them shared and set suitable caps.

For a funded client, **Take back** reclaims the entered amount. **Back to shared balance** changes the funding mode; read the confirmation before continuing.

## Set a monthly cap

Enter a positive whole number under **Monthly cap**, then click **Save cap**. Leave it blank for no cap. A cap does not add credits or reserve them from the shared balance.

Example: a funded client with 800 credits and a 1,000-credit cap can spend at most its available balance. Adding more credits still does not allow spending beyond the remaining monthly cap. A monthly cap reset does not replenish an allocated balance.

## Read the Spending tab

[![Monthly client consumption broken down by project](/img/howto/clients-spending-guide.png)](/img/howto/clients-spending-guide.png)

**Spending** shows actual credit consumption for the displayed month, with a project breakdown. Grants and transfers are excluded. It is not the client's invoice, revenue or agency profit.

If generation is blocked, check both the funding balance and the remaining cap, as well as the product's plan limits.
