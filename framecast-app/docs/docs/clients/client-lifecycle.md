# Pause, archive or offboard a client

Lifecycle actions are on the client's **Overview** tab. Choose the action based on whether access is temporarily paused or the relationship is ending.

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| Pause access | Members lose access while the client is inactive; projects and allocated credits remain |
| Archive client | Preserves work and credits, removes active access, and frees an active client slot |
| Restore access | Reactivates a paused or archived client; subject to applicable plan limits |
| Offboard and return credits | Reclaims unspent allocated credits, revokes invited memberships and delivery links, cancels pending reviews, and archives the client |

[![Offboarding confirmation describing the credits and access changes](/img/howto/clients-offboard-guide.png)](/img/howto/clients-offboard-guide.png)

*Select a screenshot to view it at full size. Screenshots use illustrative demo data.*

Before offboarding, complete any needed delivery and review the confirmation. Projects remain available to the agency. Restoring an offboarded client does **not** recreate revoked memberships; invite people again deliberately.

To find an archived client, open **Clients** and enable **Show archived**. Open its detail page to inspect the retained work or restore access.

For a temporary pause, do not offboard merely to stop generation. Use [funding and caps](./funding-and-spending) to control spend, or Pause access to suspend the relationship without revoking all memberships.
