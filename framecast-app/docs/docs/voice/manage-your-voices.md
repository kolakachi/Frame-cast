# Manage your voices

The **Voices** page is your library of **cloned voices** — voices you've created from a sample. The built-in library (expressive and standard voices, plus personalities) lives in the editor's [voice picker](/voice/pick-and-direct-a-voice); this page is for managing the voices you clone.

## Add a cloned voice

Click **＋ Clone a voice** to record or upload a sample. A clean, single-speaker clip of about 10–20 seconds — no music or background noise — works best. Full steps are in [Clone your voice](/voice/clone-your-voice).

## Where your voices are used

Once cloned, a voice is selectable anywhere you pick a voiceover:

- In the [editor's voice picker](/the-editor/voice), under **Your voices**.
- As a [channel's](/channels/create-a-channel) default voice.
- As a [brand kit's](/brand-kits/create-and-apply-brand-kits) default voice.
- In the [New Video wizard](/make-a-video/from-a-brief).

## Remove a voice

Use the ✕ action on a voice card and confirm. Scenes that already use it keep their audio; you just can't pick it for new scenes.
