# Create a series

A **series** is a recurring show. Every episode is a normal video, but it automatically inherits the series' **bible** (concept, tone, rules), **visual identity**, **production defaults**, and a rolling **memory** of past episodes — so you never re-specify format and tone, and your show stays consistent.

## Start the wizard

From **Series**, click **+ New Series**. The setup runs in five steps.

![the New Series wizard on the Basics step](/img/howto/series-create.png)

### 1. Basics

- **Series name** *(required)* — e.g. "60-Second Finance".
- **Description** — one line.
- **Channel** — attach to a [channel](/channels/create-a-channel), or keep it a **standalone series**.
- **Target platforms** — TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, X / Twitter.
- **Aspect ratio** — 9:16 (vertical), 16:9 (wide), or 1:1 (square).
- **Episode duration** — 30s, 60s, 90s, 2 min, 3 min, or 5+ min.
- **Posting cadence** *(optional)* — Daily, 3× per week, Weekly, or Custom.

### 2. Series Bible

This is the persistent context the AI receives **every time** it generates an episode.

- **What is this series about?** *(required)* — the concept in one sharp sentence.
- **Target audience**.
- **Tone** — Authoritative, Conversational, Informative, Entertaining, or Inspirational.
- **Pace** — Fast-paced, Balanced, Measured, or Intense.
- **Episode format template** — the scene-by-scene structure to follow every episode.
- **Always include / Never include** — tags the AI must honor or avoid.
- **Memory window** — how many past episodes the AI sees (0–10, default 3).
- **Auto-summarise completed episodes** — when on, each finished episode is summarized and fed into future ones for continuity.

### 3. Visual Identity

Anchors the look of every scene.

- **Visual generation mode** — Stock clips, AI-generated images, or Mixed.
- **AI image style** — Cinematic, Editorial, Vivid, Documentary, Minimal, Neon / Cyber, Warm / Earthy, or Corporate.
- **Colour palette** — Night Orange, Deep Blue, Forest, Ember, or Mono.
- **Visual style description** — free text added to every scene's visual prompt.

### 4. Characters

Characters come from your workspace [Characters](/characters/create-a-character) library and are used when generating AI images, keeping your on-screen talent consistent across episodes.

### 5. Production Defaults

Overridable per episode in the editor.

- **Voice** — the default voiceover.
- **Music** — None, Auto-match to tone, or Always instrumental — with a **music volume** level.
- **Primary language** — English, French, Swahili, Portuguese, Arabic, or Spanish.

Click **Create Series** to finish. You land on the series page, ready to generate your first episode.
