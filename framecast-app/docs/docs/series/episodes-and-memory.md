# Episodes & memory

Open a series to manage it. The detail view has two tabs: **Episodes** and **Series Bible**.

## Generate an episode

On the **Episodes** tab, click **+ New Episode**:

1. *(Optional)* give the episode a **title**.
2. Describe **what this episode is about** — the prompt for this one episode.
3. Click **Generate Episode**.

Everything else — tone, format, visual style, voice, language, and the **episode number** — is inherited from the series automatically. You're taken to the generation progress view, and the finished episode appears back on the Episodes tab.

## Episode status

Each episode row shows a status: **Draft**, **Generating**, **Ready**, **Published**, or **Failed**. Click a row to open it in the [editor](/the-editor/editor-overview), where you can refine it like any other video.

## Episode memory

This is what makes a series feel like a real show rather than a set of one-offs:

- When **Auto-summarise** is on, each completed episode gets a short summary.
- The last **N** summaries (your **memory window**) are fed into the next episode's script, so it can build on what came before and avoid repeating itself.

## Edit the Series Bible

The **Series Bible** tab shows your concept, audience, format template, always/never rules, and memory window. Click **Edit** to update any of them — changes apply to future episodes.

## Series and channels

A series can be **standalone** or **bound to a channel**. When it's bound, episodes are created under that channel, so they inherit the channel's brand and appear alongside its other videos on the [calendar](/calendar/how-the-calendar-works).
