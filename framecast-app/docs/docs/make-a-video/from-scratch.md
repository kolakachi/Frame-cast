# Start from scratch

Choose **Start from Scratch** when you want full control — no AI script, no pre-filled scenes. You pick a format and drop straight into the editor to build each scene by hand.

**Best for:** hand-crafted edits, re-creating an existing video, or when you already have all your assets.

## 1. Open the New Video dialog

From the dashboard, click **New Video** (top right), then choose **Start from Scratch**.

:::info 📸 Screenshot
`img/howto/new-video-path-picker.png` — the "How do you want to start?" dialog with the three cards.
:::

## 2. Set up your project

You'll land on **Set up your project**. Fill in:

- **Character** *(optional)* — attach a reusable [character](/characters/create-a-character) to keep on-screen talent consistent. Choose **No character** to skip.
- **Voice** *(optional)* — pick the default voiceover for the project, or leave it on the default voice. You can change this per scene later.
- **Channel** *(optional)* — assign the project to a [channel](/channels/create-a-channel). Picking a channel auto-fills its brand kit, language, and format.
- **Language** — English (US), Spanish, or French.
- **Format** — the aspect ratio: **9:16** (vertical), **1:1** (square), or **16:9** (widescreen).
- **Target length** — 30s, 60s, 90s, or 3 min.
- **Title** *(optional)* — leave blank and WyvStudio names it for you.

There's no credit estimate here — a blank project doesn't generate anything until you build scenes.

## 3. Create the project

Click **Create Project →**. You go straight to the [editor](/the-editor/editor-overview) with an empty project, ready to [add your first scene](/the-editor/work-with-scenes).
