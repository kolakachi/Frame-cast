# Generate from a brief

**Generate from a brief** is the most popular way to start. You pick a niche and a source, WyvStudio writes the script, breaks it into timed scenes, and pre-fills the visuals, voice, captions, and music — then drops you in the editor to refine.

**Time:** ~90 seconds of setup · **Credits:** estimated and shown before you generate.

## 1. Pick a niche

Each niche pre-configures sensible defaults — visual style, voice tone, captions, and music. Pick the card that fits your content, or choose **Other / Custom** to define your own.

:::info 📸 Screenshot
`img/howto/brief-niche.png` — the "Quick Start" niche grid with the Other / Custom card.
:::

### Using a custom niche

Choose **Other / Custom** and, on the content step, you'll get an editable niche panel:

- **Niche name** — what your content is about.
- **Visual style** *(optional, multi-select)* — Cinematic, Clean, Premium, Minimalist, Documentary, Dark & moody, Vibrant, Retro, Luxury, Bold, Natural.
- **Voice tone** *(optional, multi-select)* — Confident, Warm, Expert, Energetic, Calm, Authoritative, Friendly, Dramatic, Playful, Serious, Inspirational, Conversational.
- **Music mood** *(optional, multi-select)* — Calm luxury, Upbeat, Dark, Inspiring, Chill, Dramatic, Energetic, Ambient, Corporate, Cinematic, Uplifting.
- **Describe your niche** — a sentence or two so the AI understands your angle.

Leave any of these blank to let WyvStudio choose. You can select more than one option in each list.

## 2. Choose your source

Pick where the video comes from — a prompt, a script, a URL, images, a product description, audio, video, or a CSV of topics. Each source is explained in **[Choose a source](/make-a-video/choose-a-source)**.

:::info 📸 Screenshot
`img/howto/brief-source.png` — the "Choose your source" tiles.
:::

## 3. Enter your content and confirm settings

On the content step you provide your source content (see [Choose a source](/make-a-video/choose-a-source)) and set everything else:

- **Character** *(optional)* — attach a [character](/characters/create-a-character) for consistent on-screen talent.
- **Voice** *(optional)* — pick the voiceover, or use the default.
- **Visual type** — how scenes are illustrated:
  - **Stock Video** — real clips matched to each scene.
  - **Stock Images** — editorial stills and montages.
  - **AI Images** — generated frames in a style you choose (reveals a style grid — Photorealistic, Cinematic, Anime, 3D Animated, Documentary, and more, plus **✦ Custom** for a free-text style).
  - **Audiogram** — audio-reactive bars, ideal for podcasts and narration.
- **Channel** *(optional)* — assigning a [channel](/channels/create-a-channel) auto-fills its brand kit, language, and format.
- **Language** — English (US), Spanish, or French.
- **Format** — 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9. (Publishing platform is taken from the channel, if you picked one.)
- **Target length** — 30s, 60s, 90s, or 3 min.
- **Title** *(optional)* — leave blank and WyvStudio writes one for you.

The **estimated credit cost** shows at the bottom, along with your balance. If you're short, the button becomes **Top up credits →**.

:::info 📸 Screenshot

:::

## 4. Generate

Click **✦ Generate Video**. You'll watch the pipeline write the voice, create visuals, add motion, and compose music, then land in the [editor](/the-editor/editor-overview).
