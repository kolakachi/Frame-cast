# Choose a source

When you [generate from a brief](/make-a-video/from-a-brief), you pick a **source** — where your content comes from. WyvStudio reads whatever you give it and turns it into a scene-by-scene video. Here's each source and what to provide.

## Write a Prompt

Describe what the video is about in a sentence or two. WyvStudio writes the full script and breaks it into scenes.

> Best for starting from an idea with no existing material.

## Paste a Script

Paste your script — each paragraph becomes a scene. A checkbox controls how much WyvStudio changes it:

- **Let AI polish my script** — **off** keeps your exact words; **on** lightly improves flow and hook. It's **off by default**, so your words are used verbatim unless you opt in.

> Best when you've already written your narration and want to keep it.

## From URL / Article

Paste any article or page URL. WyvStudio extracts the main body text and structures it into scenes.

> Best for turning a blog post, press release, or landing page into a video.

## Upload Images

Two modes:

- **Upload reference images** — add 1–15 images (JPG, PNG, or WEBP, up to 10 MB each). WyvStudio builds the video around them.
- **Generate AI B-roll** — pick a visual style and describe the video; WyvStudio generates matching visuals.

A short context box lets you say what the video should be about.

> Best for product shots, screenshots, or a set of stills you want to animate.

## Product Description

Fill in **Product name**, **Product URL**, **Description**, and **Target audience**. WyvStudio writes a product-focused script.

> Best for DTC ads and product launches.

## Upload Audio

Upload a recording (MP3, WAV, or M4A, up to 500 MB). WyvStudio transcribes it and structures it into scenes.

> Best for turning a podcast clip or voice memo into a short.

## Upload Video

Upload existing footage (MP4 or MOV, up to 2 GB). WyvStudio extracts and repurposes it into a new short.

> Best for repurposing a long video into clips.

## CSV Batch

Paste multiple topics as CSV — one per line, in `topic,angle,hook` form — to set up several videos at once.

> Best for producing a batch of videos across many topics.
