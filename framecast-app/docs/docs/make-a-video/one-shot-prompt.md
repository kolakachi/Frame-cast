# One-shot from a prompt

**One-shot** is the fastest path: type one line (or paste a product URL) and WyvStudio drafts a full plan you can tweak, then generates the whole video — visuals, motion, voice, captions, and music.

## 1. Describe your video

In the composer, type what you want — for example:

> A calm founder explaining her morning ritual, ending with a call to visit the site.

Or paste a URL: *"5-scene ad for https://yourproduct.com."* The prompt box holds up to 8,000 characters. Press **⌘/Ctrl + Enter** to draft the plan.

## 2. Set the controls

Under the prompt, use the pills to shape the output. Anything you don't touch is inferred from your prompt.

- **Visual source** — **AI images** (generated per scene), **Stock video** (Pexels clips), **Stock images** (Pexels stills), or **Audiogram** (waveform over the voice). Stock and audiogram are free.
- **Aspect ratio** — 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, or 16:9.
- **Scenes** — 1 (fastest demo), 3 (DTC ad shape), 5 (narrative), or 8 (full Reel, ~40s).
- **Animate** *(AI images only)* — off, or a motion tier. Each shows its credit cost:

  | Tier | Engine | Cost | Render |
  |---|---|--:|---|
  | Wan 2.5 | fast, cheap | 50 cr | ~30s |
  | Seedance Lite | ByteDance, cheap | 30 cr | ~45s |
  | Hailuo 2.3 | best for most | 35 cr | ~90s |
  | Seedance Pro | ByteDance, sharp | 125 cr | ~2 min |
  | Kling 2.1 | cinematic | 100 cr | ~3 min |
  | Spokesperson | lip-sync, talking | 130 cr | ~3 min |

- **+ Add** *(AI images only)* — upload up to 4 reference images (max 8 MB each) or pick a [character](/characters/create-a-character) to guide the look.

## 3. Review the plan

Click **Review plan →**. WyvStudio drafts every scene — the script line, plus an expandable image (and motion) prompt. Nothing is generated or charged yet. Here you can:

- Edit any **script** line or **image/motion** prompt.
- Toggle **On-screen captions** and **Background music** (both on by default).
- Check the estimated **credit cost** for the run.

:::info 📸 Screenshot

:::

## 4. Generate

Click **⚡ Generate**. WyvStudio builds all scenes and drops you in the [editor](/the-editor/editor-overview) when it's done.
