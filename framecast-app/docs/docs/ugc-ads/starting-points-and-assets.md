# Starting points, formats and assets

Your **brief input** and **starting point** answer different questions. An idea, exact script or product URL supplies the message. The starting point tells the app how to use source material.

| Starting point | Use it when | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| From scratch | You want a new ad from a brief or script | Product facts and the proposed creative direction |
| From an ad I like | You want inspiration from a video's structure | Read the source first and review the extracted structure; do not assume its footage or words will be reused |
| From my own footage | You have clips to build around | Select the actual files and check which clips the plan uses |

For a reference ad, choose a video and use the reading control before planning. If reading fails, try another source or describe the structure yourself. For owned footage, choose files you are entitled to reuse; uploading a clip and selecting it for this ad are separate actions.

## Choose a format

Expand **More direction** to choose a format, or leave **Let the director choose** selected.

| Format | Suitable for |
|---|---|
| Continuous talking take | A presenter delivering a focused message |
| Product / app demonstration | Explaining a product or showing how something works |
| Story with deliberate cuts | A narrative with intentional changes of shot |
| Silent reaction + headline | A short facial reaction with the message carried by a headline; choose 5 or 10 seconds |
| Text-led cards | An ad led by stills and on-screen words, without a presenter |

Do not put “no cuts” into a brief that also asks for several changing camera angles. Keep the instructions consistent with the format.

## Product photos are not footage

Product reference slots accept **stills**, not video clips. Add up to five clear product photos: the front label, another useful angle, and a view that shows scale. Avoid several nearly identical images.

Use the footage controls for video. A reference file does not guarantee an exact reproduction: inspect logos, labels, proportions and product handling in the generated result. Use real product footage where exact demonstration details matter.

## Select the right client first

If producing for a client, open that client's workspace before starting. Save its [client brief](/clients/the-client-brief), then check the assets and facts in this specific ad's plan. A file in the client library is available context; it is not a promise that every generation will include it.
