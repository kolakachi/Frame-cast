# Review, revise and export



The screenshots below demonstrate the review interface where enabled. Watch the completed video from beginning to end before downloading or sending it to a client.

## Review the result

Check the spoken words, product facts, pronunciation, face and hands, product label, timing and call to action. The scene preview may differ from the composed export: captions and music are composed into the exported video.

## Request a change

1. Choose the relevant change category: **Script wording**, **Voice delivery**, **Footage**, **Captions** or **Timing**.
2. Select the passage if the take has more than one editable passage.
3. Describe a specific change: “Shorten the closing sentence to ‘Explore the Acme range’” is clearer than “Make it better”.
4. Click **Preview changes & cost**.
5. Read the proposed actions and cost, then approve them if correct. Preparing the proposal is not the same as applying it.
6. Wait for the revision to finish and review the new result.

[![A revision proposal with its affected action and a separate credit approval](/img/howto/ugc-revision-guide.png)](/img/howto/ugc-revision-guide.png)

*Select a screenshot to view it at full size. Screenshots use illustrative demo data.*

The proposal shown uses demo data; your actual actions and price depend on the requested change.

Do not submit the same request again while it is running. If a revision reports partial failure, inspect its status/history before requesting another change.

## Export the current version

Use the export action under **Happy with it?**, then wait for **Export ready**. Download the completed file and check it once more.

[![Export-ready controls for download, scheduling, approval and sharing](/img/howto/ugc-export-guide.png)](/img/howto/ugc-export-guide.png)

After changing the take, export again. An earlier export is not automatically updated by a revision. Sharing, scheduling and **Send for approval** depend on a completed export of the current work.

For client work, follow [Review and approve a version](/clients/review-and-approval), then create a [delivery package](/clients/delivery-and-handoff). A public share link, a review link and a delivery link serve different purposes.
