# The dashboard

You land on the **Dashboard** after signing in. It opens with a welcome and your credit balance.

## Set up your studio

**Set up your studio** walks you through four steps:

1. Add your brand.
2. Save how your brand name is said.
3. Add your face and voice.
4. Make your first video.

Nothing is locked, and you can hide the card anytime.

## This week

Once the setup steps are done, that card gives way to **This week**: a Monday–Sunday strip from your [calendar](/calendar/how-the-calendar-works).

- Each day shows its posts as **title · platform** pills.
- **Green** is posted, **orange** is scheduled, **red** failed.
- A day shows up to 3 posts, then **+N more**.

## Recent videos

**Recent videos** shows your latest videos, each with a real frame from the video.

## Low credits

When you have about 3 videos' worth of credits or fewer, a low-credit line appears. Otherwise it stays hidden. See [Account & billing](/workspaces/account-and-billing) to top up.

## Start a new video

To start a video, open **Classic › Composer** or **Classic › UGC Ads** in the sidebar. See [Create your first video](./create-your-first-video).
