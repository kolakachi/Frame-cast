# Manage channels

Open **Settings › Channels** and click any channel to open its own page. The page has four tabs.

## Tabs

- **Overview** — the channel's brand setup (brand kit, voice, language, platforms, format, status) and its most recent videos.
- **Videos** — every video in the channel, with its source type, variant count, and status. Open a video or jump to its variants from here.
- **Brand** — a read-only summary of the channel's brand defaults.
- **Settings** — edit the channel's name, description, platform targets, language, brand kit, and default voice. Save with **Save**.

![the channel detail view with its tabs](/img/howto/channel-detail.png)

## Edit a channel

Open the channel and use the **Settings** tab. Update any field and save.

## Archive a channel

In **Settings → Danger Zone**, choose **Archive** and confirm. Archiving removes the channel from your active list — **existing videos remain accessible**. It's an archive, not a permanent delete.

:::note
New channels can no longer be created from the app. See [Where channels are now](/channels/create-a-channel).
:::
