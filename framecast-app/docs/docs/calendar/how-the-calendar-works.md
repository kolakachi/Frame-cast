# How the calendar works

The **Calendar** (Publishing Calendar) is where everything you schedule and publish shows up. Use it to see what's going out when, fix failures, and manage drafts.

## Views

Switch between three views at the top:

- **Month** — a month grid; each post shows its platform icon, title, and time.
- **Week** — a week with hourly slots.
- **Posts** — a filterable list of every post.

Navigate months/weeks with the arrows, or jump back with **Today**.

![the month view with scheduled and published posts](/img/howto/calendar-month.png)

## Statuses

Each post is color-coded:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled** (blue) | queued to publish at a set time |
| **Published** (green) | live on the platform |
| **Pending / Processing** (orange) | publishing right now |
| **Draft** (gray) | saved, not scheduled |
| **Failed** (red) | publishing didn't complete |

If anything fails, a banner links you straight to the failed posts. The calendar auto-refreshes while posts are publishing.

## Filters

- **Platform** (Month/Week) — toggle YouTube and TikTok to focus the view.
- **Series** — filter to a single series (when you have them).
- **List tabs** (Posts view) — All, Scheduled, Published, Failed, Draft, each with a count.

## Manage a post

Click any post to open its details, then:

- **Edit** *(scheduled, draft, or failed)* — change the caption (and YouTube title), and the date/time for scheduled posts.
- **Retry** *(failed)* — re-attempt publishing.
- **Cancel** *(scheduled, draft, or failed)* — remove it.
- **View** — open the live post on the platform (once published).

## How posts get onto the calendar

Posts come from the **Schedule** action after you [export a video](/the-editor/export-share-and-schedule) — you pick accounts, write captions, and choose to schedule, publish now, or save as a draft. See [Connect & publish](/export-and-publish/connect-and-publish) for connecting accounts and the full scheduling options.
