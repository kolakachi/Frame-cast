# Account & billing

Everything about your account, connected platforms, and billing lives in **Settings**.

## Account

**Settings → Account**:

- **Name** and **Timezone** — editable. **Email** is shown but not editable here.
- **Preferences** — Auto-generate captions, Preview before render, Auto music, and Watermark on free exports.
- **Password** — set or change your password (minimum 8 characters).
- **Your data** — download a JSON export of your data, or delete your account.

## Connected accounts

**Settings → Connected Accounts** links the platforms you publish to:

- **YouTube**, **TikTok**, and **Instagram Reels** (Facebook Reels is coming soon).
- Click **Connect** and authorize in the popup. If a token expires, you'll see a warning to reconnect.
- **Disconnect** removes an account — any scheduled posts using it are cancelled.

These accounts are what you pick when you [schedule a post](/the-editor/export-share-and-schedule).

## Usage & billing

**Settings → Usage and Billing** shows your current plan, credit balance, and usage bars.

### Plans

On the free plan you can upgrade to a subscription — **Starter ($19)**, **Creator ($39)**, **Pro ($79)**, or **Agency ($149)** — via secure checkout. Once subscribed, **Manage Billing** opens your billing portal.

### Credit top-ups

Buy one-time **credit packs** when you run low mid-cycle. Top-up credits don't expire while you have an active paid plan. (On an [AppSumo lifetime deal](/), your bucket is a one-time grant — buy top-ups the same way when it's used up.)

### Credit history

The **Credit history** table breaks down where credits went — by operation (script, image, animation, voice, export, grants) — with filters for spent vs. added and a time window.

### Refer a friend

Share your referral link to earn credits when someone you refer converts.
