# Manage your workspace

Your **workspace** holds everything you make — projects, channels, characters, voices, brand kits, and assets — along with your plan and credit balance.

## Rename your workspace

Open **Workspace** and click the name to edit it inline. The workspace name appears in the sidebar and across the app.

## Track usage

The Workspace page shows how you're tracking against your plan:

- **Credits** — your current balance (the real limit on what you can generate). A banner warns you when it hits zero, with a link to top up.
- **Exports** — renders used vs. your limit.
- **Voice Minutes**, **Voice Clones**, **Dub Languages** — usage against plan limits.
- **Channels** — active channels vs. your limit.

An **Overview** row summarizes totals — Projects, Assets, Completed Exports, and Brand Kits.

:::info 📸 Screenshot
`img/howto/workspace-usage.png` — the workspace usage cards and overview.
:::

:::info Credits are the limit
Credits are only spent on AI generation (images, video/animation, voice, music). Writing scripts, breaking scenes, stock matching, and exporting are included. When you run low, [top up](/workspaces/account-and-billing) — top-up credits don't expire while your plan is active.
:::
