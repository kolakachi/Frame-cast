# Video variants

**Variants** let you produce many alternate cuts from one finished video — perfect for testing which hook, voice, or format performs best. Open them with the **Variants** button in the editor top bar.

## What a variant is

A variant is an alternate cut derived from a base project. Variants are produced in **variant sets** — one batch, many outputs — and each can be opened in the editor or exported like any other video.

## Generate variants

Click **Generate Variants** and choose what to vary:

- **Hook** — generate 2–5 different openers. (You need at least two [hook options](/variants-and-hooks/generate-hook-variants) generated first.)
- **Voice** — pick one or more voices to try.
- **Visual** — refresh scene visuals with new matches.
- **Format** — render in multiple aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9).

**What stays fixed:** your brand kit and template are locked. You can choose to lock or unlock **scene text** and **captions**.

A live **batch summary** multiplies your choices — e.g. *3 hooks × 2 voices × 1 visual pass × 4 formats = 24 variants* — so you can see how big the batch is before you run it.

![the Generate Variants drawer with dimensions and the batch summary](/img/howto/variants-generate.png)

## Review and export

Each variant becomes a card showing its status, which dimensions changed (e.g. "Alt hook · Onyx voice · 1:1"), and per-card actions to **Open**, **Download**, or **Delete**.

- Select the variants you want and click **Export** to render them (use **Select All** / **Clear All** to move fast).
- **Retry Failed** re-runs any that didn't render.
- **Queue Detail** shows per-variant progress. The page refreshes automatically while a batch is running.

## Localized versions

The **Localized Versions** section spins your video into other languages — Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Hindi, Japanese, and Arabic. Pick languages and click **Generate**; localized cuts appear as their own cards to open or retry.
