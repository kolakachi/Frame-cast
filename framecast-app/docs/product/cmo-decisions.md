# CMO-2: the four decisions

Step 2 of `cmo-brief.md`, drafted 2026-10-17 from `cmo-evidence.md` (§ numbers below point there). Each decision says
what it is, the evidence for it, and what would prove it wrong. These are recommendations until the owner accepts them;
the customer emails (§8) may change them. Nothing here is built or published yet.

## 0. Before any decision matters: fix the first session

Half the AppSumo refunders never made a video of their own (§3), and every channel below sends people into the same
first session. Three fixes come first, because sending traffic to a leaky first session wastes all of it:

1. **No empty forms, and no blank editor for newcomers.** 6 buyers started with Classic's "Start from scratch" (an empty
   editor); 5 refunded. Guard the product form against empty submits, and lead newcomers to the first-ad box or a
   template instead.
2. **No sample about us.** Every new workspace gets an auto-made "Wyvstudio Ad" about WyvStudio. Replace it with the
   buyer's own first ad: paste your product link (or pick a template), get your ad.
3. **New buyers start in Create or a template, not Classic.** All four buyers who used Create are still active; the
   refunds were almost all Classic (§1, §4).

Measured by FUNNEL: share of new sign-ups with a video of their own in the first session (target 80%).

## 1. The buyer

**Small sellers who run their own ads and posts:** an owner or a one-person marketing role at a small business that
sells one thing and has to keep promoting it (an online or local shop, a solo brand, an author, a coach, a service
business), posting short video on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, with no shoot budget and no team.

| Evidence | Where |
|---|---|
| Store owners and small business owners are 35 of 58 market quotes; their words are "creatives die", "can't keep up", "can't afford" | §7 |
| Our buyers who stayed all have something ongoing to promote: a lottery shop, a health brand, a book, a coaching programme, daily channels | §2 |
| Our buyers target TikTok (47 projects), then Reels | §2 |
| Buyers with a one-off need (a campaign, an IT plan, a pitch) refunded | §2 |
| Same pain, less money in emerging markets: ₹20k a month in India, ₦100k salaries in Nigeria, "I don't know how to make ads, I hate recording videos" in Brazil | §7 |

**Not the buyer (for now):** large DTC teams with creative budgets, side-hustlers chasing "AI income", one-off
campaigns. **Second segment worth testing:** small agencies whose local clients "can't be bothered to film" (§7), who
buy for many sellers at once.

**Proved wrong if:** after 100 sign-ups from this group, fewer than 5% pay, or nobody buys a second time within 60
days; or the customer emails say they don't need new videos at least monthly.

## 2. The offer

**Your first ad free, made from your product link. After that, pay once: no subscription.**

- Free: one ad from a template with their product, words and colours; costs us cents (no AI builder). Watermarked;
  download allowed.
- Then: the $9 Test Pass, then packs (Starter $47, Creator $157, Agency $299). One-time, no subscription.
- Every video is planned and priced before anything is made, and checked before they see it.

| Evidence | Where |
|---|---|
| Every competitor sells a subscription; pay-once is open ground | §6 |
| Competitors open with a tiny first step ($1 first ad, 3 free videos, 3-day trial), so a free first ad is the market's price of entry | §6 |
| "can't justify a $500+/mo retainer", "burning cash", "I carry 100% of the financial risk" | §7 |
| Emerging-market budgets suit one-time packs | §7 |
| Direct buyers (who saw the price up front) refunded 0 of 4; AppSumo buyers 10 of 19 | §1 |

**Proved wrong if:** fewer than 5% of free first ads lead to a purchase after 100 free ads; or direct refunds rise
above 10%; or buyers ask for a subscription.

## 3. The message (messaging house)

**One line:** *Fresh ads before your old ones die.*

**Under it:** *Paste your product link and get ready-to-post video ads of your real product, checked before you see
them. No shoot. No subscription.*

| Pillar | The pain it answers (their words, §7) | Proof we can show |
|---|---|---|
| **Fresh ads every week, without a shoot** | "Our winning ads die after 7-10 days"; "can't keep up"; creators at "$200-300/video, wait 7-10 days" | A product link becomes an ad in minutes; a template ad costs cents, a full Create ad about $2–11 of credits (115–592 credits at Starter pricing) |
| **Your real product, checked** | "screams generated"; "It can't (easily) get my products right"; "transforms the item"; "AI slop" | We use their own product photos; every video is checked before delivery; faceless ads with no fake people, presenters only when they want one |
| **Pay once** | "can't justify", "burning cash", "carry 100% of the financial risk" | One-time packs from $9; the price is shown before anything is made |

This replaces nothing yet: the locked statement in `positioning-roadmap.md` stays until the owner accepts this one. The
brand story's belief ("every small business deserves the ads big brands have", `brand-story.md`) sits behind it.

**Proved wrong if:** in message tests (two hooks boosted at $5–20 each, a headline A/B on the template pages, 5-second
tests) the "ads die" line loses to a plain "make ads from your product link" line; or customers, in their replies,
describe us in different words.

## 4. The main channel (Bullseye)

**Main: the presenter's short videos on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.**

Bullseye ranking from the evidence:

| Ring | Channel | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Inner (test now) | **Presenter short videos** | Our buyers make TikTok content, so they are on TikTok (§2); competitors' main motion is short video, most of it through creators (§6); every video is also product proof |
| Inner (test now) | **Community answers** where people post "my ads die" (r/FacebookAds, r/smallbusiness, r/socialmedia) | The exact pain moment, in public, free (§7) |
| Inner (test now) | **OUTREACH-5:** a real ad made for 5 sellers a week | Proves the offer one person at a time; costs time, not cash |
| Middle | Template pages and articles (search, AI answers) | Compounds slowly; runs in the background |
| Outer | Shopify App Store, paid creators, Google Ads | Need build time or cash |

**The rule:** the presenter is the main channel and is judged by volume (100 videos); community answers and OUTREACH-5
are fast, cheap tests run beside it for two weeks, to learn which words and which sellers respond. If one of them clearly
beats the presenter on free first ads per hour of work, it becomes the main channel instead.

**Proved wrong if:** after 100 presenter videos, fewer than 50 free first ads came from them; or in two weeks community
answers or outreach bring more free first ads for the same effort.

## 5. The owner's decisions (2026-10-17)

| Decision | Owner's answer |
|---|---|
| The buyer (§1), with agencies as a second test | **Yes** |
| The offer (§2): free first ad, watermarked, download allowed, then pay once | **Yes** |
| The one line and three pillars (§3) | **Yes**; they replace the locked positioning statement once the message tests agree |
| The presenter | **Layla**: one of our stock presenters (character 101, "Middle Eastern woman in her late twenties, warm honey skin, thick dark wavy hair, dramatic eyes, glam makeup"). Three pilot videos with a cost estimate first, to check her face holds |
| Markets | **Emerging markets are a target, and so are the US and Europe, where our current buyers are.** Pay-once suits both: no monthly commitment anywhere. Outreach and communities cover both; the product and site stay global English |
