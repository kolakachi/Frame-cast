WyvStudio · 20 s launch promo

PROPOSAL · S9 CHANGE…

Change… sits next to Download on the finished video. Its menu names the parts this video has: Words, Voice, Look, and Pictures only when the video has generated people or shots. Picking one opens the plan drawer at that part, filled with what the video uses now. Editing it and pressing Plan the change sends the edits as one change request: the usual plan card comes back with the price of only what changes, and everything else (voice, music, pictures, footage) is reused. Something else just focuses the chat box. Nothing is charged for opening the drawer.
