PROPOSAL · S9 CHANGE…

Change… (next to Download) opens the drawer listing the parts this video is made of: its pictures and clips, logo, footage, words, voice, music and look, each with what you can do and its price on the button. Change this moment appears on the player whenever it is paused: it grabs that frame and its time and adds it to the drawer, with what is said there, the sounds near it and the music there, for anything that is not a single part (layout, text, motion, a line, a sound). Music, voiceover and sound effects as a whole are a Sound group: changing a whole track re-times the video. Every edit is collected in the drawer; Plan the change sends them as one change. The plan card then shows the price of only what changes, and unchanged parts are reused. Nothing is charged until that plan is approved; opening the drawer and swapping in your own files are free.
