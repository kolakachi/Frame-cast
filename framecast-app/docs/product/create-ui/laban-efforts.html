Laban efforts

Give every beat a named motion quality (Punch, Glide, Float…) chosen for what the beat means, and turn each name into exact animation settings. Today a video has one timing for everything; this gives each moment its own character and tells the builder how hard, how fast and along what path things move.

Proposed, not built About 1 day to build No new screens for users Source: “The 8 types of movement according to Laban Movement Analysis”
The idea

Three dials, each one an animation setting

Laban described every movement by its effort on three factors. Each factor turns straight into a setting our builder already writes. (A fourth factor, Flow, is how freely a move follows through; it sits out of the eight actions and becomes our settle: precise stop or loose follow-through.)

How it moves through Create

Four places change, none of them on screen

Planner

Picks an effort per beat

The eight presets join the motion voices in the planner's catalogue. Each beat gets effort {id, why}; an unknown id is dropped, never guessed.

Builder guidance

Gets the settings, not the theory

The pinned motion guidance lists each beat's effort with its numbers. A new WM.effort(tl, el, "punch") recipe in wyv-motion.js plays it, so the builder writes one line, not a hand-tuned tween.

Motion voice

Still sets the tempo

The video's voice scales every effort's duration and overshoot, so a calm brand's Punch is still a punch, just a slower one. Effort is the character; voice is the tempo.

Checks

Advisory notes for the builder

Two checks read the timeline that actually ran (we already record which moves ran). Notes for the builder only; users never see them.

Voice × effort

The same effort in two voices

Durations scale by the voice's entrance time against the steady default (0.5 s). Overshoot uses whichever is larger: the effort's or the voice's.

Effortcalm_seriousbold_playful
Punch0.31 s · scale 1.250.13 s · scale 1.25

Float2.24 s · 1.000.96 s · 1.12
Dab0.22 s · 1.050.12 s (floor) · 1.12

A floor of 0.12 s and a ceiling of 2.4 s keep any pairing watchable. Today's rule “nothing moves at constant linear speed” stays; efforts make it concrete, because every preset has an ease that accelerates in and settles.

Checks

What gets measured

advisory · effort_repeat

Three beats in a row with the same effort

Closes part of FS3 (“the same technique repeated”). The planner is told to vary; the check catches a build that flattened them anyway.

advisory · effort_mismatch

The beat's main move doesn't match its effort

A Punch beat whose main entrance takes 0.9 s, or a Glide with a 1.3× overshoot. Read from the recorded timeline (duration, ease, path), so it's measured, not guessed from frames.

unchanged · blocking stays as is

No new blocking check

Efforts improve the motion; they never stop a delivery. The blocking checks (blank frames, reading time, names said as saved, planned moves) stay exactly as they are.

Scope

What would be built

  • FormatPlaybooks.phpThe eight presets (weight, time, space, duration, ease, path, scale, impact, use-for) next to MOTION_VOICES, and in the planner's catalogue.
  • PlanService.phpscenes[].effort {id, why} in the planner's schema; normalised and passed to the build like motion_voice. A change in the Change drawer keeps every beat's effort unless the change is about that beat.
  • wyv-motion.jsWM.effort(tl, el, id, {at, to, voice}): the eight presets in GSAP, scaled by the motion voice; indirect efforts use a generated arc or wave path.
  • composition-agent.mjsGuidance: each beat's effort and its settings, pinned with the motion kit; “build the beat's main move with its effort”.
  • delivery checkseffort_repeat and effort_mismatch (advisory), from the recorded moves.
  • tests + proofUnit tests for the presets, normalising and both checks. Then rebuild two GTM-1 briefs (#4 Ledgerly, #10 3 hooks) with efforts, about 500 credits, for your side-by-side verdict against the versions you already judged.
Your call

Three decisions before building

1. Do users see the efforts?

The plan card could show a plain word per beat (“lands hard”, “glides in”), or keep it internal.

Recommend: internal for now. Users already say plans look technical; “make this moment punchier” in the Change drawer would map to an effort behind the scenes.

2. Use efforts to direct people in generated takes?

Gemini Omni takes could get “gestures with a Press quality: slow, firm, deliberate”.

Recommend: not in this slice. Test it separately on one UGC brief once the motion side is proven.

3. Add the Laban video to the exemplar library (O6)?

It sets the quality bar for from-scratch explainers: one colour per section, a giant ghost word, one abstract object acting out the idea, live sliders.

Recommend: yes, and use “a 60 s explainer of the 8 Laban efforts” as a from-scratch test brief once efforts are built.
