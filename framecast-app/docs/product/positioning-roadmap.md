# Positioning roadmap: lean brand & marketing teams

Owner decision, 2026-10-10. Replaces the May 2026 "site stays broad" rule and the "Create short videos from whatever you have" headline.

## Who

Brand and marketing teams of 1–20 people running paid social, mostly ecommerce and app/SaaS brands. They need new ad videos every week and have no shoot budget or agency.

**Statement (locked 2026-10-10):** *WyvStudio makes short videos for your ads and social posts from your product, with or without a presenter. You approve the plan and price first, and every video is checked before you see it.*

- **What we do:** we make the video.
  - Teams download it and upload it to Meta or TikTok Ads Manager themselves.
  - Or they publish and schedule it as an organic post from WyvStudio.
  - We never run ads. Copy must not say or imply that we do ("ready-to-run", "launch your ads").
- **Formats:** presenter videos (UGC) and faceless videos (product shots, motion graphics, voice-over, built in Weave) are equal. Neither is the identity of the product.
  - Faceless is often the better ad: cheaper per video, quicker to make variants of, and no "is this person AI?" doubt.

## Why: homepage review (respond.md)

- The page talks to too many audiences: brands, agencies, creators, affiliates.
- There are too many ways in, and the features compete with each other. (Owner: the list of ways in stays; it just shouldn't be the headline.)
- Weave and WyvStudio are never explained, so visitors confuse the two names.
- The headline is generic. The differentiator ("show us an ad you love") is buried.

## Phase 2: proof (weeks 2–6)

- [ ] **Outbound offer:** a free ad made from the team's own product.
- [ ] **Case studies:** turn the best results into 2–3 short ones with real numbers.
- [ ] **First session:** the main goal is that the first visit ends with a finished ad.
  - 8 of 18 AppSumo sales were refunded, so this matters more than more traffic.

## Phase 3: product depth for this customer (ongoing)

- [ ] **VISION:** the director sees product photos (from the link or an upload).
- [ ] **Variety:** hook variants, and one-click export in every size (P2-ASPECT).
- [ ] **Batches:** weekly ad batches, the habit a lean team needs.
- [ ] **Pricing check:** a team that needs ads every week fits a monthly plan better than one-time packs.
  - Keep the public page one-time for now.
  - Once about 10 teams buy a second pack or top up, bring back one monthly brand plan. That repeat purchase is the signal.

## What to measure

| Metric | Tells us |
|---|---|
| Homepage visit → sign-up | The message lands |
| First finished ad in the first session | The product delivers |
| Second purchase or top-up within 30 days | There is a weekly need, so monthly may be due |
| Refund rate | Whether expectation matches reality |

Only reopen the positioning if these stall after real traffic. Otherwise, ship the next item on the list.
