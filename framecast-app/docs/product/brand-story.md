# WyvStudio brand story

Draft for the owner, 2026-10-17. After the "organic reach from zero" post: a brand is one big story told in chapters, and
the product roadmap is the story roadmap. This page is what every post, page, email and presenter video draws from.
`positioning-roadmap.md` keeps the locked positioning statement; `strategy.md` keeps the plan. Nothing here is final
until the owner edits it.

## 1. The story in one line

**Every small business deserves the ads big brands have.** WyvStudio is the creative team they couldn't afford.

## 2. The answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who are we? | We make ads for your product, from a link, a script or an idea, without a shoot. |
| Where did it start? (the "before") | Small businesses lose to big brands on one thing: big brands post fresh ads every week. A shoot costs more than a small shop makes in a month, and an agency more than that. So the small shop runs the same ad until it stops working. |
| What do we believe? | Every small business deserves the ads big brands have. An ad should be planned, priced and checked before you pay, not fixed after. Variety wins: the platforms now reward many different ads, not one good one. |
| Who is it for? | Small businesses and lean teams that need fresh ads every week, wherever they are. Many are in markets the big tools ignore. |
| Why us? | Video, not still images. A plan and a price before anything is made. Every video checked before you see it. Templates that make a finished ad in minutes, and a Creative Director for when you want something new. |
| Where are we going? (the end) | **Your own creative team: every week, fresh ads that sell, made, checked and improved from what worked.** |
| What's the payoff? | WyvStudio. |

## 3. The beliefs, in order

Each piece of content moves the viewer one step along this chain. A post that doesn't serve one of these is off-story.

1. **One ad isn't enough any more.** Platforms show ads to people who look like the ad's audience; ten versions of the
   same ad reach the same people ten times. You need variety: different people, reasons and looks.
2. **Shoots can't keep up.** Variety every week is impossible with cameras, creators and agencies on a small budget.
3. **AI can, if it's checked.** AI makes the variety affordable; a plan you approve and checks before delivery make it
   trustworthy.
4. **So your ads should come from WyvStudio.** One product link in, a week of different ads out.

## 5. The presenter

WyvStudio's face is an AI presenter, made in WyvStudio and openly AI (strategy.md §4; rules in study.md §4).

**Her job in the story:** she is the creative team member every small business wishes it had. She shows, she doesn't
sell: every video makes something real, live, for a real kind of product.

**Personality (proposed):** warm, quick and practical, a little cheeky about bad ads, never smug about customers.
Think the friend who works in advertising and will look at your ad over coffee.

**Name:** to choose. Short, easy to say in many languages, not a real person's name.

**How she talks:**
- Plain words, short sentences, the customer's own words (from the customer-words bank once built).
- Shows the result in the first 3 seconds, explains after.
- Says what something costs and how long it takes.
- Says "WyvStudio" as "weave studio".

**She never:**
- Pretends to be human, a customer or the founder, or tells a made-up life story.
- Says or implies WyvStudio runs ads or guarantees sales.
- Mocks a customer's business; she can tease a bad ad, never the person.
- Compares WyvStudio by name to avatar tools.
- Mentions Series.
- Gives a testimonial; real customers' words, with permission, are the only proof she quotes.

## 7. Packaging rules

- **Hooks**, one of: a bold claim ("this ad took 4 minutes"), a callout ("shop owners running the same ad since
  summer"), a curiosity gap, a clear outcome, a contrarian line ("you don't need a better ad, you need twelve").
- **Titles:** the promise and the outcome, nothing to decode.
- **First 3 seconds:** the finished ad on screen, the hook as on-screen text.
- **Native to each platform**, and scannable everywhere.
- **Outliers:** when a post does 10× normal, make five more like it.
