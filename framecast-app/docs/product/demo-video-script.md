# WyvStudio demo video: script v1 (2026-10-14)

About 81 seconds, 16:9, for the AppSumo listing and YouTube. Modelled on the Memogram demo's anatomy (presenter frames
the story and the ask, the product does the proving, one voice runs the whole way) and on the classical order of a
speech: opening, story, turn, proof, answering doubts, close. Every claim is something the product does today.

**One voice, start to finish.** The master voice track is made first (ElevenLabs v3, one call per beat with the beats
before and after passed as context). Presenter beats are animated from that beat's own audio (OmniHuman 1.5, one image),
placed muted at the beat's exact start. Everything else plays under the same voice: L-cuts out of the presenter, J-cuts
back in. Music under everything, lowered while she speaks. Captions follow the master track.

**Pronunciation.** WyvStudio is said "weave studio": the TTS text says "Weave Studio", captions show WyvStudio.

## Presenter: clothing and background

- **Clothing:** solid mid-tone colours; no fine stripes, checks or small prints (they shimmer in generated video); no
  logos or text. Smart-casual, a marketer at work: a soft knit or a relaxed blazer over a plain top. One warm tone that
  sits beside WyvStudio orange without copying it (rust, terracotta, camel). Small earrings only. Identical in every clip.
- **Background:** a real working space with depth: a softly blurred studio or office, shelves, a plant, a warm lamp,
  window light from one side. No screens or readable text behind her, no other people. Neutral and warm, with one orange
  accent object. Clear separation between her and the background. The same place in every clip.
- **Framing for OmniHuman:** a 16:9 image, waist-up, eye level, facing the lens, mouth closed, hands visible and relaxed
  (for gestures), room on one side for captions and the logo.
