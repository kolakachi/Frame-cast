# Scope: verify, don't claim · teaching arc

Two pieces of work, scoped 2026-10-05 after a day of reliability failures on the UGC path (eight distinct faults on
one brief) and the owner's review of two prompts (the Opus 5.5 "story-led educational animation" prompt and Nate
Parrott's motion-editor spec). Nothing here is built until the owner approves; decisions are marked **Decide**.

## 1. Verify, don't claim

**The rule (from the editor spec):** nothing is called working until it has been run. Today every fault was found on
the owner's credits: none of our suites put real-world media through the real render path.

**What exists:** `scripts/reliability.mjs` (cancel, timeout, a failed render keeps the last good file),
`scripts/acceptance.mjs` (forward and reverse seeking give the same frames), the API and worker unit suites. All use
one tidy fixture with no video clips, no long footage and no generated media.

### 1a. The media gauntlet (free, every worker or render change)

A new sandbox script, `scripts/gauntlet.mjs`, run through the same `live-tool.mjs render` path production uses.
Fixtures are generated by ffmpeg at start (no files in git, no paid calls):

| Fixture | Stands in for |
|---|---|
| 5 min, 1920×1090, 60 fps, a keyframe every 4.3 s, no sound | today's demo screen recording |
| 4 s, 720×1280, 24 fps, one keyframe, with sound | an Omni or Seedance clip |
| 2–4 s cuts with keyframes 2–4 s apart | the builder's own ffmpeg cuts |
| an odd-sized `.mov` and a `.webm` | footage users upload |
| a clip with sound but a silent first 3 s | a take that starts late |

The composition uses six clips at once (two cut from 200 s or more into the long source), zoomed 1.8×, with captions,
music, the sound pass and a 60 fps variant. Asserts:
- it renders `ready` inside the deadline, at the right size, length and frame rate, with sound;
- **determinism:** the same composition rendered twice gives the same frames at 3 times (decoded pixels equal);
- **preview equals export:** a HyperFrames snapshot at a time matches the exported frame at that time (pixel diff
  under a threshold);
- **headroom:** peak memory and process count stay under 70% of the sandbox limits (today's hang was the process
  limit; memory peaked at 97%);
- intake: every fixture passes `VideoIntake` and `makeRenderable` and comes out renderable; silent stays silent.

Runtime target: under 5 minutes. Output: a short report (pass or fail per assert, times, peaks).

### 1b. Seam tests (free, in the API suite)

Each of today's faults becomes a test at the seam where it happened, so it cannot come back unnoticed:

| Fault | Test |

### 1c. Provider contract check (free, daily)

Replicate publishes each model's input schema. A scheduled command, `create:provider-contracts`, reads the schema of
every engine we route to (Seedance 2.5, Omni, Veo 3.1, Kling, the first-frame engines, ElevenLabs music, Gemini TTS)
and compares it with what our adapters send: parameter names, allowed values (aspect ratios, durations), required
fields. A difference is logged and shown on the admin health page before a user hits it. It does not catch rules that
are not in the schema (Omni's "image or references, not both" was not); those stay with 1b and the canary.

### 1d. Paid canary (needs a budget)

**Decide:** a standing budget for three real briefs run end to end through the app on the test account, after each
deploy and weekly: UGC from a reference with a demo link, footage plus motion graphics from scratch, motion graphics
exact. About 1,500–1,800 credits a round (about $6–7 at the credit peg; today's clean UGC exact run was 531, the DistroKid run about 988, a motion-graphics build 200–300). This is the only test that
catches what free tests cannot (a provider changing behaviour, a model refusing a shot). Recommended: yes, after each
deploy only, not weekly, until the bench runs.

### 1e. The ledger

`create:failures {--since=}` lists every failed or held run with its stage, cause (from the trace), credits spent and
whether a later run of the same creation succeeded; a weekly count goes in the progress tracker. Free.

### 1f. Network (from today)

Every provider call (model gateway, listening, transcripts, downloads) rides out about 30 s of DNS failure with backoff;
local containers get fixed DNS servers in a local-only override (production untouched). Free.

## 2. Teaching arc and transitions (planner and checks, free to build)

### 2a. The teaching arc

For explainer, tutorial and how-it-works briefs (the planner decides from the brief; marked on the plan as
`format: explainer`), the beats follow the arc: **hook question → familiar world → disruption → mechanism → discovery →
consequence → recap**. Short videos compress it: 15 s uses hook, mechanism, discovery and recap; 30 s uses all seven.
Each scene gets `arc` (its step). The rest of the prompt's learning design comes with it: concrete before technical,
one new idea per beat, ask before each reveal, labels only when the object cannot explain itself.

Not for ads, UGC or exact copies (they keep the reference's or the brief's own structure).

**Decide:** show the arc step on the plan card under each beat (one small label), or keep it planner-only.
Recommended: planner-only for now.

### 2b. A transition on every beat

Each scene gets `transition_out`: what on screen becomes the next scene and how, e.g. `{"from": "the upload button",
"becomes": "the progress ring", "move": "morph"}`, or `{"move": "cut"}` where a cut is right (UGC, a reference that
cuts). The taste rule already asks for this; the field makes it a requirement the build is checked against: a named
move must be built (the existing move check), and the plan is sent back if a transition names something that is not
on screen in that beat.

### 2c. Three checks from the prompt

| Check | Where | Blocking? |
|---|---|---|
| Every beat has its own job (no two beats with the same reads) | plan lint, sent back to the planner | yes, at planning |
| The opening question is answered by the final image (explainer format) | final look, with the hook's question | advisory |
| On-screen text does not repeat the narration word for word (explainer format; captions and UGC exempt) | build check, comparing timed text with the narration's words | advisory |

### 2d. Proof

Re-plan two explainer briefs (Opus, about $1.25 each), build one locally, and check the arc, the transitions and the
three checks on the result. The motion-graphics regression (H1) is re-run to show ads and exact copies plan as before.
