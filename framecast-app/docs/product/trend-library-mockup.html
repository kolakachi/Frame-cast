The weekly job, then three places it is read

Runs every Monday at 03:00 UTC on the scheduler. Each step is cheap; the planner only reads a few hundred words from a table, so a video costs nothing more.

First, the pilot (half a day)

One manual week for two categories, 10 in-house briefs planned with and without patterns, blind-rated by the owner. The weekly job is built only if pattern hooks win.

What changes
  • One line on the hook, only when a pattern was used.
  • "Why this hook" shows the examples: views and lift, no links out to other people's posts inside the app.
  • The toggle replans without the block, same planning price.
When there's no line
  • No category match (e.g. a B2B software brief outside the 7).
  • That week's patterns are older than 14 days.
  • The user wrote their own hook.
