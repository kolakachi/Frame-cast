# Weave release notes (opened to all customers 2026-10-09)

Moved into `api/config/changelog.php` on 2026-10-09 (three entries: Meet Weave, Start from the dashboard, Weave in ChatGPT and Claude) when Weave opened to all (CREATE_OPEN_TO_ALL). Still to do: the public docs pages listed at the end.
Pronunciation: WyvStudio is said "weave studio".

## new · Start a video from the dashboard
Pick the kind of video you want (a product ad, a launch teaser, a UGC testimonial, an explainer, quick tips, or one
like a video you love) and fill in one short form: the voice (hear each one first, including your cloned voice), the
presenter for UGC, the details, length, screen, captions, style and effort, and any photos or logo from your library.
Continue, and Weave starts planning straight away. You see the plan and its price before anything is made.

## new · Script Writer
Describe the product in a line and Script Writer drafts a voiceover sized to your video's length, shaped for the kind
of video you picked. It only uses what you wrote and your brand name; anything it would have to invent, like a price
or an offer, is left in [brackets] for you. Free, up to 20 a day.

## new · Next videos for you
Once you have made a video, the dashboard suggests three more: a new angle on your last one, a kind of video you have
not tried, and a quick one for an empty day this week. Start this opens it ready to go.

## new · Start from a video you love
A new video in Weave now shows our example videos under the box. Hover to watch, filter by kind, and press Make one
like this: Weave studies its pacing, cuts, layout, look, motion and sound and makes yours with your product, words,
brand and voice. The ? next to the heading explains how close it can follow (exactly, similar or inspired).

## improved · Weave asks before it guesses
The first plan says the length, screen and voice it was made for, so you can keep them by approving or change them
in a word. If you have not saved how any name is said, it asks; reply "Dewbloom is pronounced …" and every voice
says it that way from then on.

## improved · Tuned for your business
The dashboard orders the kinds of video, and words their examples, for what your business sells.

## new · Weave in ChatGPT and Claude
Ask your AI assistant for a video and it uses Weave: weave_start plans it, weave_status shows the plan, weave_approve
always shows you the price first and only builds after you agree, weave_reply answers Weave's questions or asks for
changes, and weave_share gives you a link. (Docs: docs/product/api-weave-mcp-guide.md has the tool details for the
public mcp-tools page.)
