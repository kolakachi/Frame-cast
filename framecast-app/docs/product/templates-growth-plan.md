# Templates as a growth loop, inside the CMO plan

Plan for the owner, 2026-10-17. Owner: "we can turn this into something: multiple templates users can use or get
inspiration from", and "I don't want us to deviate from our recent CMO plans". So this page only puts templates to work
inside the experiments already in `cmo-plan.md` (Oct 19 to Nov 1). Anything new waits for the Nov 1 review. Nothing here
is built until the owner says so.

## 1. What the CMO plan already says

- **FREE-FIRST** (experiment 5): 20 free first ads in two weeks; keep it if 2 or more purchases follow. Free ads are
  made from templates, so templates are its supply.
- **Stays out for these two weeks** (§5): "template pages beyond what FREE-FIRST needs". So the public template gallery
  (TPL-PAGES in the todo) does not start now.
- **Tracking** (experiment 1): every link carries `utm_content` = the post or the template.
- **Layla, community answers, OUTREACH-5** (experiments 6, 7, 9): each needs a real example ad to show.

## 2. Templates in each experiment (no new surface)

| Experiment | What templates do | Work |
|---|---|---|
| FREE-FIRST | Enough free templates that most small sellers find one that fits: the 8 being built now (drop, sale, three reasons, problem then fix, review card, app, local shop, food), each audited, plus fixes to the near-clean ones | Under way (`template-audit.md`) |
| Tracking | Each post, answer or email links to a template's free ad with `utm_content=<template id>`, so the scoreboard shows which templates bring sign-ups | One small link change, below |
| Layla pilot | One of her 3 pilot videos shows a template becoming a real seller's ad ("this took 4 minutes"); the link opens that template | Content only |
| Community answers | An answer to "my ads die" shows a free template ad made for a product like theirs, with the link | Content only |
| OUTREACH-5 | Each of the 5 ads is made from a template (already the rule in the todo), and the message links to that template's free ad | Content only |

**The one small build:** a link that opens one template's free ad directly:
`wyvstudio.com/…?free=1&template=<id>` signs up free and lands on that template, with the UTM kept. It is what
FREE-FIRST needs for every link above, so it stays inside the plan. About an hour, plus tests.

## 3. Parked for the Nov 1 review

Decided at the review, from the scoreboard (free ads per template, sign-ups by `utm_content`, free to paid):

| Idea | Why later |
|---|---|
| **Public template gallery** (TPL-PAGES): indexable page per template, video, what you can change, "Make this free" and "Use as inspiration" | Out by §5; worth it if template links bring sign-ups |
| **Organised by what sellers sell** (food, beauty, apps, local, drinks) and by goal (launch, sale, reviews) | Needs more templates than 2 weeks will make |
| **A weekly template drop** (a few new ones a week, each a Layla post and an email line) | Judge after the pilot shows whether template posts get views |
| **"Make one like this"** as the paid path from a template a free user can't swap (Similar or Inspired on the Test Pass) | Already possible in the app; promote it only if free ads convert |
| **Templates from what works** (most used, bought again) | Needs weeks of data |

## 4. Decisions for the owner

| Decision | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Keep the gallery (TPL-PAGES, SHARE-TPL) out until Nov 1, as `cmo-plan.md` §5 says | Yes; move them from "Now" to the review in the todo |
| Build the direct template link (§2) now | Yes; small, and FREE-FIRST's links need it |
| Use templates in Layla's pilot, community answers and outreach as above | Yes; content, no build |
