> anything is made. No subscription.

### Long description (about 120 words)

> WyvStudio makes short video ads and branded videos for small businesses, without a camera or a shoot. Paste a link to
> your product, add a photo, or start from a ready-made ad template, and WyvStudio's Creative Director plans the video in
> your brand: the concept, scenes, script, voiceover and music. You see the plan and the price before anything is made,
> and every video is checked before you see it. Change it with a message, make it again for another product, or make a
> UGC-style ad with a presenter. Your first ad is free; after that you pay once for credits, with no subscription. It
> works in the browser, and inside ChatGPT and Claude.

### Features (bullet list, where asked)

- Video ads from a product link, a photo, a script or an idea
- Ready-made ad templates: your words, colours and photos in a finished ad
- The plan and price shown before anything is made
- Every video checked before you see it
- Changes by message, version by version
- UGC-style ads with presenters
- Vertical, square and landscape
- Share a watch link or post to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook
- Works inside ChatGPT and Claude
