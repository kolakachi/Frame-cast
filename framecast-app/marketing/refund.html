WyvStudio Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026  ·  Effective: 23 September 2026

14-day money-back guarantee. If WyvStudio is not right for you, email us within 14 days of your purchase and we will refund it in full — provided you have not already used a substantial part of the credits it included. Bought through AppSumo? Their 60-day guarantee applies instead; see section 4.

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to purchases made directly from WyvStudio through our checkout:

  • One-time plans (Starter, Creator, Agency) — a one-time credit pack with no monthly refill
  • Credit top-up packs
  • The UGC Test Pass
  • Monthly subscription plans, including renewals

Purchases made on AppSumo are covered by section 4 instead.

2. The 14-day window

You may request a refund within 14 calendar days of the purchase date. For subscription renewals, the window runs from the date of the renewal charge, not the original sign-up.

3. Credits you have already used

Credits pay for AI generation — writing scripts, synthesising voices, generating images and rendering video. When you spend a credit we pay an external provider to do that work, immediately and irreversibly. We cannot get that back, so a refund cannot cover generation you have already had.

In practice this means:

  • If you have used less than 20% of the credits included in the purchase, we refund it in full.
  • If you have used more than 20%, we are unable to refund that purchase, because a substantial part of what you bought has already been delivered.

Ask us and we will tell you exactly how many credits your workspace has used before you decide. Credits granted free — at sign-up, through a promotion or as a goodwill adjustment — are never counted against this, and are not refundable in themselves because you did not pay for them.

4. AppSumo purchases

Lifetime deals bought on AppSumo are covered by AppSumo's own 60-day money-back guarantee, not by this policy. Request those refunds through AppSumo, who processed the payment — we cannot refund an AppSumo order from our side. Once refunded there, the corresponding WyvStudio access ends.

5. Discounts and promotional pricing

A purchase made with a discount code or during a promotion is refundable on the same terms as any other, and is refunded at the amount you actually paid. A discount does not shorten the window or remove your rights under this policy.

6. How to request a refund

Email hello@wyvstudio.com from the address on your account, with your order ID or the email used at checkout. You do not need to give a reason. We aim to reply within 2 business days and to process approved refunds within 5 business days.

7. How refunds are issued

All payments are processed by Kelviq, our Merchant of Record. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Funds typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer. We refund in the currency you were charged; we are not responsible for changes in exchange rates or fees applied by your bank.

8. What happens after a refund

When a refund is issued, the purchase is treated as though it had not been made:

  • Any credits remaining from the refunded purchase are removed from your workspace.
  • A refunded one-time plan or subscription returns to the free tier; paid features and limits end.
  • Videos you have already exported remain yours to use. We do not revoke a licence to work you created.

9. When we cannot offer a refund

  • Requests made more than 14 days after the purchase date.
  • Purchases where more than 20% of the included credits have been used (see section 3).
  • Free credits, promotional credits and goodwill grants, which were never paid for.
  • Accounts suspended for breaching our Acceptable Use Policy, or purchases we reasonably believe to be fraudulent or made to abuse this policy — for example repeated buy-use-refund cycles across accounts.

If you dispute a charge with your bank rather than contacting us, we may suspend the account while the dispute is resolved. We would always rather sort it out by email first.

10. Your statutory rights

If you are a consumer in the UK or EU you have a statutory right to cancel a purchase of digital content within 14 days. That right ends once you ask us to begin supplying the content immediately and we do so — which, for WyvStudio, is when you first generate with your credits. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot be waived under the law that applies to you, and where our terms are more generous than the law, ours apply.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our products change. The version in force is the one published on the date of your purchase, and we will not apply a later, less generous version retroactively.
