How it works

  1. Start with whatever you have — a one-line idea, a full script, a product URL, a PDF or an audio file.
  2. Review the plan. You see the scenes, the visual direction and the cost before anything is generated.
  3. Edit anything you like — swap a visual, rewrite a line, change the voice, restyle the captions.
  4. Export in every aspect ratio you need, and publish or schedule it straight to your accounts.

What you get for $69
