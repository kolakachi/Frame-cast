Questions

Do I need to film anything?

No. Product photos or a product link are enough. If you want your own face in the ad, one clear photo is all it takes.

Will the product look right?

WyvStudio uses your own product photos as the reference for every shot and checks the result before you see it. Still look closely at labels and packaging before you run an ad, as with any AI-made footage.

Do I have to say the ad is made with AI?

Meta and TikTok both ask advertisers to label realistic AI-generated people. Use their AI label when you upload, and never present an AI presenter as a real customer with a real review.

Can I use my own voice?

Yes. Record a short sample once, and your cloned voice can speak in any ad. Starter includes one cloned voice and Creator includes two.

Do I own the ads?

Yes. You keep ownership of the videos and scripts you make and can run them anywhere.

Is there a subscription?

No subscription is needed. Every pack on this page is a one-time payment. Monthly plans are also available inside the app for teams that prefer them.
