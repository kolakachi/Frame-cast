The alternatives, one by one

WyvStudio pay once

Bring product photos or a product link and a sentence. The Creative Director writes the script, picks the presenter and scenes, and shows you the plan and the exact price before anything is made. It covers presenter ads such as testimonials, unboxings and before-and-afters, and faceless ones such as ASMR and POV hooks. You can use one of 60+ presenters, your own face from one photo, and your own cloned voice.

Pick it if you want to pay once rather than subscribe, or you want both UGC ads and non-presenter product videos in one place. Skip it if you need dozens of variations generated in one batch from a product link, which is Creatify's strength.

Creatify

Paste a product page and Creatify generates several avatar ads at once, built for testing many variations. It has the cheapest entry plan in this list.

Pick it if you want volume from a product link on a small monthly budget. Its lowest plan has a limited number of credits each month.

HeyGen

The best-known AI presenter tool, strong on lip-sync, your own avatar and translating one video into many languages.

Pick it if your ads are mostly a person talking to camera, or you sell in several languages. It is less focused on casual, phone-shot UGC.

MakeUGC

The closest to Arcads in approach: choose an AI actor, write a script, and generate creator-style ads, with a focus on product ads.

Pick it if you want the Arcads workflow for less. Check the regular price as well as the Black Friday one.

Higgsfield

A creative suite with many video and image models, aimed at polished, cinematic generated footage rather than a ready-made UGC ad workflow.

Pick it if you want to experiment with models and visual styles, and you are comfortable assembling the ad yourself.

When Arcads is still the right choice

Arcads is built for performance marketing teams that produce many actor-led ads every week and have the budget for it. If you already rely on its actor library and workflow, and price isn't your main concern, switching may not be worth it. The alternatives above matter most to small brands and lean teams for whom a large monthly bill is hard to justify before an ad has proved itself.

Questions

What is the cheapest Arcads alternative?

For a monthly plan, Creatify at $15 a month ($10 billed yearly). To pay once with no subscription, WyvStudio's $9 Test Pass makes one complete UGC ad with a presenter, and its packs start at $47.

Is there a free Arcads alternative?

Creatify and HeyGen have free plans with watermarked exports, and WyvStudio's first ad is free, made from a template with a small watermark. For ad-ready, unwatermarked UGC, every tool here needs a paid plan or pass, because generating realistic presenters costs real compute.

Which alternative is best for product-in-hand ads?

Both MakeUGC and WyvStudio focus on ads around a real product. WyvStudio builds every shot from your own product photos, which helps the packaging look right. Whichever you choose, check labels and logos in the result, because AI can alter small details.

Can I use my own face and voice instead of an AI actor?

Yes, in WyvStudio (one photo for your face, a short recording for your voice, both included from Starter up) and in HeyGen (custom avatars). Use only faces and voices you have the rights to.

Do AI UGC ads need to be labelled?

Meta and TikTok ask advertisers to label realistic AI-generated people. Use the platform's AI label, and never present an AI actor as a real customer with a real experience.
