Questions you'll have before you sign up.

Do I need editing experience?

None. If you can describe what you want in a sentence, you can make a video. You change things by asking, not by editing a timeline.

Can I start from a video I already like?

Yes. Paste a link or upload the clip, and Weave builds yours in its pacing, type and motion, with your brand and your words. It never reuses the original's footage, characters, logos or words.

Can I use my own face and voice?

Yes. Upload one photo and your character stays the same across every scene and every video. Clone your own voice once and use it in any video. Both are included from Starter up.

Will it say my brand name right?

Yes. Tell it once how your name is said, and every voice says it that way. The finished audio is checked for it before you see the video.

Can I change one part without starting over?

Yes. Ask for a new voice, a different line or a tighter ending, and only that part changes; the rest stays exactly as you approved it. Every earlier version is kept, so you can go back to any of them.

Do I need a product to sell?

No. Start with an idea, a service you offer, or an affiliate product you're authorised to promote. Add who it's for, and Weave builds the video around one clear message and call to action.

Can I post straight to my accounts?

Yes. Connect YouTube, TikTok and Instagram (Reels and Feed), then schedule from one calendar that tracks every channel. Included from Starter up.

What do credits pay for?

Making the video: the plan, the script and voice, the visuals and the render. A typical 20-second video uses about 230 credits, plan included; a UGC ad with a presenter uses more. You see the price in the plan before anything is made, and you can top up any time from $8.

What's the refund policy?

Backed by a 14-day money-back guarantee. Full terms are in our Refund Policy.
