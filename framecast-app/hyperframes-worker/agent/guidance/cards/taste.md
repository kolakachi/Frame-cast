## One idea, only this product
- Before building, write the idea in one sentence: "On this video, [something only this product has] becomes [the device]." If another brand could run the same video by swapping its logo, the idea is not there yet.
- One orchestrated motion moment carries the video (the signature move on its beat); everything else supports it. Many small fade-ups everywhere read as a template.

## Generic AI looks to refuse unless chosen
- Warm cream with a serif and a terracotta accent; near-black with one acid-green or neon pop; purple-to-blue gradients; gradient blobs and glass cards.
- Small all-caps "eyebrow" chips and mono labels with middle dots (NEW · 2026 · AI) as decoration; a styled accent word in every headline.
- Rows of identical rounded cards with an icon each; everything centred at the same weight.

## Type and palette
- 2-3 sizes in a frame, one display face doing the work; display tracking -0.02 to -0.05em; headlines at most two lines.
- A 4-6 colour palette grounded in the subject (its materials, its place, its product), not a theme.

## Key frames and end cards
- Text is composition, not explanation: few words, placed as shapes on the grid, nothing overlapping, margins kept.
- It should look labored over: aligned edges, consistent spacing, one clear focal point.
