# Launch and promo films

## Story spine (one arc, one job per beat)
- Hook 0–2 s: one tension-maker that moves within 2 s: a stat counting, a jab whose key word swaps, a typed question, a cold open on the product doing its trick. Never a logo first.
- Turn 2–5 s: the pain or the "imagine", 3–5 short lines landing one at a time, no product yet.
- Reveal: the name or mark lands once (logo-sting / cta-morph into the UI); then 2–3 feature beats on the same real surface, each feature → its benefit → one proof (a UI result, a number from approved_facts, a logo wall). Never stack raw features.
- Close ≥ 2 s held: one action, message-matched to the hook ("save 2 hours" hook → "Save 2 hours, try free"), via cta-lockup / cta-close, then the mark or URL. The last beat echoes the first.
- Arcs: PAS (pain→agitate→intro→proof), Future pacing (imagine→name→mechanism→outcome), Demo loop, Before/After, Feature→Benefit cascade. Pick one; story truth never bends to a shape.

## Cuts (which seam for which change)
- Same idea continuing (line → next line, card → next card): cut-the-curve, both sides moving the same way, exit power4.in 0.2–0.4 s + entry power4.out ≥ exit, partial travel ~230 px and fade, never fully off-screen.
- New chapter (hook → context): zoom-through, scale 1→1.2 blur 0→10 px power3.in 0.2 s, hard swap at peak, incoming 0.75→1 expo.out 0.5 s. Payoff or arrival: inverse zoom-through (outgoing recedes to 0.8, incoming lands from 1.25). Text blur 10 px, full-frame surfaces 18–20 px, on the wrapper, same both sides.
- Word-level line change: waterfall cut (cut-the-curve per word, staggered). Kit: WM.whip for a chapter, WM.push/wipe for a step, WM.giantWipe once as the signature.

## Motion language
- Smooth beats bouncy: power3 long-tail (WM.ease.heavy/default) is the default; overshoot only when the brand is playful.
- Reveal to the voice: at t=0 only what is being said; each line, card or stat enters on its spoken word (transcript timings), spread over the back half of the beat; then hold still. Front-load-then-freeze is the slideshow failure.
- No lazy breathing, no slow pan in the back half of a beat; no motion beats bad motion. Aliveness = a tiny settle, live SVG internals, or nothing.
- Product shots: a camera push on a #camera wrapper, 2–3 parallax layers, UI state changes on the beat (toggle flips, row lands, counter climbs) with a sound each.
- Seek-safe: fromTo entrances, no repeat/yoyo, no randomness, no CSS transitions.

## Visual design
- One field per beat, dark or light, flipped on the hits; the product is the hero at ≥ 40 % of the canvas, in real chrome (browser-device-stage / device-frame-stage, real URL), rebuilt from the page capture with its real copy, never lorem, never an unframed screenshot, no fake metrics.
- ≥ 3 depth layers (ground, surface, foreground); hierarchy by two of size 3:1, weight 800/400, position (upper third), motion. Squint test: one thing dominates.
- Text economics: ≤ 6 words per on-screen line, ≤ 2 lines, one idea per beat; narration carries the rest.
- Captions ride as an overlay: one line, bottom-centred in the bottom 5–8 % (caption-highlight, pill-karaoke), the spoken word highlighted; composition centred on the true middle, never shifted up for them; keep tiny critical text (URL, legal) out of that strip.
- Never: nav bars, footers, scrollbars, real cursors, bokeh or purple "AI" gradients, decorative shapes in place of a real asset.
