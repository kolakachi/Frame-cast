## Settle the spec before writing
- Palette by role, declared once: field (background), ink, accent (one hue, visible: 15-25% as atmosphere, full on the focal hit), support (a tinted neutral for cards and rules). Tint neutrals toward the accent; never pure #000 or #fff. No full-frame linear gradients on dark (they band): radial, or solid plus a local glow.
- One type scale from frame height H: display 12-20%, headline 6-9%, body 2.6-3.6%, label 1.7-2.2%. Nothing under 24 px. Hero text spans 60-80% of the width.
- 12-column grid, outer margin 5-7%. Pin content to edges or zones (left panel / right content); centred-and-floating is a web layout.
- Three layers per beat: background treatment (glow, ghost type bleeding off-frame, panel, grid, grain), midground message, foreground accents (rules, labels, data bars, mono metadata). Decorative opacity 12-25%, borders 2-4 px, padding 60-140 px; 6-10 visual roles in a produced frame.

## Typography
- Two families at most: one performs, one recedes. Serif + sans or sans + mono, never two sans. Weight contrast extreme: 300 vs 900, not 400 vs 700.
- Display tracking -0.03 to -0.05em; light-on-dark reads heavier, so body 350 and line-height +0.05. Lines of 5-7 words; 3 s on screen must read in 2.
- Shipped: Anton, Bebas Neue (display), Playfair Display (serif), Space Grotesk (techy sans), Inter (quiet body), Caveat (handwritten). Columns of numbers: tabular-nums.
- Time is hierarchy: what appears first matters most; how a word enters is part of its typography.

## Composition
- One hero per frame, at least 3x any support element. Two focal points so the eye travels; the second anchors an edge.
- Thirds and asymmetry: hero off-centre, bleeding off an edge, air on the other side. Negative space is a layer.
- Rules, dividers and panels guide the eye and animate well (scaleX 0 to 1). Never a raw flat image: tilt + shadow, slow zoom, a device frame, or a piece floating at its own depth.
- Refuse unless chosen: gradient text, left-edge accent stripes, cyan-on-dark, identical card grids, everything centred at equal weight.

## Looks (pick one, name it)
- Swiss Pulse: dark field, one cobalt accent, numbers at 80-120 px, grid-locked, hard cuts. SaaS, data.
- Velvet Standard: near-black, thin wide-tracked caps, symmetry, long holds, glides. Luxury.
- Deconstructed: raw, scan lines and grain, glitch cuts, type as texture. Dev, security.
- Maximalist Type: huge layered type and colour blocks, 0.4 s cuts. Announcements.
- Data Drift: thin sans, particles and light traces, extreme scale shifts. AI.
- Soft Signal: warm light field, soft gradient and grain, slow eases. Wellness.
- Shadow Cut: deep shadow, vignette, grain, cinematic reveals. Drama.

## Beats
- Direct each beat as an experience: a concept, a motion verb per element (SLAMS, SLIDES, DRAWS, FLOATS, TYPES ON; no verb, not designed), depth layers, a sound cue.
- One event per beat; state_in and state_out concrete, every on-screen word verbatim. Beats 1.5-3.5 s; a 5-7 word line gets 2-2.5 s; first visible motion within 0.2 s.
- Name the rhythm before building (fast-fast-SLOW-fast-HIT-hold): one held beat where nothing moves and the line lands, the signature move on its beat, one seam direction.
- Resolve colour and type from the explicit brief and approved plan, respecting explicit brand locks. A kit, capture or saved style supplies defaults, not an unconditional override. Borrowing reference motion and borrowing its palette are separate choices. After building, check colours and type against the approved treatment.
